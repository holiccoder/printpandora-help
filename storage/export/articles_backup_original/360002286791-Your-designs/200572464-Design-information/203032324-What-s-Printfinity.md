Printfinity by MOO allows printing a different design on every card in a pack at no extra cost, ideal for showcasing portfolios, product ranges, or brand stories. Available on various products, designs are printed on the back in upload order, creating unique, mixed packs that help businesses and creatives stand out.

How Printfinity Works
---------------------

1. **Choose your product**  
    Printfinity is available on a wide range of MOO products, including Business Cards, Postcards, Stickers, and more.
2. **Upload multiple designs**  
    When creating your order, you can upload as many designs as there are cards in your pack. For example, in a pack of 50 business cards, you could upload 50 unique designs.
3. **Every card is unique**  
    Each card is printed with one of your chosen designs, giving you a pack filled with variety instead of identical cards.

Please note that, with Printfinity, all of your designs are printed on the **back,** and are printed and packaged **in the order that they are uploaded in**, they are **not sorted by design**. This means that they will be **mixed/alternating** throughout the pack.

Why Use Printfinity?
--------------------

- **Showcase your work**: Perfect for creatives, designers, and photographers who want to display a portfolio across their cards.
- **Highlight product ranges**: Businesses can use different designs to feature multiple products or services in one order.
- **Tell your story**: Share your brand personality in different ways across each card.
- **Stand out**: Handing out a business card that’s different from the next one makes you—and your brand—more memorable.

What Makes Printfinity Special?
-------------------------------

Unlike traditional printers, which charge extra for multiple designs, MOO’s Printfinity technology is included at no extra cost. This means you can be as creative and versatile as you like without worrying about the price going up.
