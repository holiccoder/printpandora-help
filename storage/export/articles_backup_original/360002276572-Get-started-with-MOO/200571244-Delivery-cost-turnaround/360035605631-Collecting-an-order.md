Collection is available from our office located in East Providence, Rhode Island.

If you would like to collect your order instead of having it shipped, all you need to do is place your order and [contact us](http://moo.com/contact) with your order reference number.

We will mark your order for collection, and refund your shipping costs (minus any expedited printing costs) and let you know your estimated pickup date along with our address details. You will receive an email once your order is ready to be collected.

**Our pickup times are Monday through Friday, 9am - 5pm EST.**
