We understand that some customers prefer to have their orders shipped to a PO Box. While our checkout process requires a physical address, we're happy to help you ship to your PO Box after your order is placed.

Why Can't I Enter a PO Box at Checkout?
---------------------------------------

Our system requires a physical address at checkout because certain carriers cannot deliver to PO Boxes. This ensures that your order is processed without delays.

How to Ship to Your PO Box
--------------------------

1. **Place your order using a physical address.**
2. **Contact our Customer Service Team** with your order number and the PO Box address you'd like to use.
    - You can reach us via [Chat, Phone, or Email](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/204288414).
3. We'll update your shipping address and ensure your order is shipped via the US Postal Service.

![Warning Icon](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/30840341635988) **Important Notes:**

- We do not offer next-day shipping to PO Boxes.
- Shipping to PO Boxes may take longer than standard delivery times.
