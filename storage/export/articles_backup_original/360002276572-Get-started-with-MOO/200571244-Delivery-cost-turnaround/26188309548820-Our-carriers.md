For orders shipping within the US, we have partnered with USPS and UPS. If you would like to know which of these carriers your order will ship with, please [contact our Customer Service team](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/204288414-How-to-contact-our-Customer-Service-team) after placing your order.

For international orders, we have partnered with USPS and DHL.
