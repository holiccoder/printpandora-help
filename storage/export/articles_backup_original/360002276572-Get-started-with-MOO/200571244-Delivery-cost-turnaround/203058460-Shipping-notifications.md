Once your order has been collected by the carrier, Little MOO will email shipping details with the tracking link included.

The tracking information along with other updates on the status of your order is available in [Your Account](https://www.moo.com/account).

If you have any issues receiving your package by the specified date, please contact our [Customer Service](http://moo.com/contact) team and we'll be happy to help.
