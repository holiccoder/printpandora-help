We have a variety of sales and promotional offers that occur over the course of the year. These vary in the discount rates offered, but we will always communicate our promotions via our MOOsLetter.

In order to stay up to date with these offers, you can sign up for our MOOsLetter [here](https://www.moo.com/newsletter-signup). We’ll send you occasional emails about things we think you’ll find useful and interesting, including expert business tips, inspirational customer stories, new product updates, and of course, exclusive discounts.

Please note that if you’ve created an account, you can unsubscribe at any time and manage how MOO communicates with you [here](https://www.moo.com/consent/manage). If you don’t have an account, clicking the unsubscribe link found within any MOOsLETTER will remove you from our list.
