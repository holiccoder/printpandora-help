Every order is reviewed by our MOO Quality team to ensure your design meets printing standards before we send the order for production.

We examine all files for printability, addressing common issues such as alignment and contrast - If there are any concerns with the artwork, the team will contact you with the next steps.

We will not review for grammatical or spelling errors. It's essential to double-check your artwork before submission to ensure accuracy.

Please note that you will have **3 hours** after placing your order to cancel or edit it. Any change or cancellation requests made after that time may not be possible. Should you wish to cancel or edit your order in those 3 hours, please email [**wecanhelp@moo.com**](mailto:wecanhelp@moo.com).

**Please note that this article refers to our Branded Merchandise products.**

**For our paper-printed products, please find product information and guidance** [**here**](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us) **and support** [**here**](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/204288414)**.**
