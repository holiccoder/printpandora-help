**We have discontinued our NFC Business Cards+ as of 31st March 2020.**

**Why was NFC removed?**

We had some challenges with paper sourcing, manufacturing, and technical development. It became more and more difficult to deliver a product that meets the high standards we want to offer customers.

**When did you stop selling NFC cards?**

NFC was removed from the site at the end of March 2020. You will no longer be able to order new NFC cards, reorder a previous purchase, or change the actions on your existing cards through MOO.

**When will my NFC cards stop working?**

**As of January 2023.** Any actions that have already been programmed will no longer work. You will not be able to change the cards’ actions using MOOs platform, instead you’ll need to do this via a third-party app.

**Can I still change the actions on my cards?**

Yes, via a third-party app. It’s quite easy to do and we’d recommend the following on iOS and Android smartphones:

- [**iOS**](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/nfc-tools/id1252962749)
- [**Android**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wakdev.wdnfc&hl=en_GB)

**Do you know anyone else that offers NFC technology?**

A quick online search for “NFC Business Cards” will bring lots of results. Although we haven’t tested every alternative or have any affiliation with another company. See above for our recommendation on apps that let you change your cards’ actions.
