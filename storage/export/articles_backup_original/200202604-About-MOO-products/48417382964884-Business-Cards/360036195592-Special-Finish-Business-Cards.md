- Add smooth, transparent shine
- Contrasts the Soft Touch finish
- Makes images and text dazzle
- Velvety Soft Touch finish

- For thick, touchy-feely shine
- Adds an extra tactile dimension
- Makes cards look and FEEL amazing
- Velvety Soft Touch finish
