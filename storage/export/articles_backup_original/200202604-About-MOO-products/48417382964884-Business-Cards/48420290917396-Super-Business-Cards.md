What are Super Business Cards?
---------------------------------

[Super Business Cards](https://www.moo.com/business-cards/super) are made from thicker 380gsm (19pt) paper, designed to feel sturdier and more premium in hand.

They’re finished with a Soft Touch matte coating, giving them a smooth, velvety feel.

  
Is this right for you?
-------------------------

This works well if you want:

- A thicker, more substantial card
- A smooth, premium finish
- Access to special finishes like foil or spot gloss

Compared to other options:

- Thicker than Original and Cotton
- More flexible in finishes than Luxe
- Less thick than Luxe, but more design-driven

What makes the paper different?
----------------------------------

Super paper is engineered for durability and a refined finish.

- **Denser and thicker** than standard paper stocks
- Designed to feel **sturdy and substantial**
- Smooth surface allows finishes like foil and gloss to apply cleanly

The Soft Touch coating adds:

- A **velvety, matte texture**
- A slightly grippy, tactile feel

This combination makes Super the most flexible option for decorative finishes.
