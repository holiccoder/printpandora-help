![MERCH-650-Paper-rich-media-4-Original-1152x576.jpg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/49515805565332)What are Original Postcards?
----------------------------

[Original Postcards](https://www.moo.com/us/postcards/original) are MOO’s feel-good premium paper option for Postcards. They’re double-sided, full color, and made to give your design a premium look and feel for less.

Choose a matte or gloss coating, then decide whether you’d like that coating on one side or both. Simple choices, with plenty of room to make them your own.

  
Is this right for you?
-------------------------

This works well if you want:

- Postcards with a premium look and feel for less
- A choice between matte and glossy finishes
- Double-sided, full-color printing
- Up to 25 different back designs per pack with Printfinity
- A range of postcard sizes, from Small to Large

Compared to other postcard paper stocks:

- Original is MOO’s feel-good premium paper, available with matte or gloss coating
- Super is stronger and more durable, with a silky Soft Touch coating and optional Gold or Silver Foil
- Luxe is extra-thick, uncoated and naturally textured, with a choice of 8 color seams

Customization
----------------

Original Postcards can be printed double-sided and in full color. With MOO’s Printfinity option, the front of every card in your pack will have the same image, and you can have up to 25 different back designs.

Printfinity options may be limited on MOO Designs.

- **Matte:** Shine free, no glare, and writable
- **Gloss:** Shiny finish that makes colors more vibrant
- **Coating:** Choose coating on one side or both sides

You can design your Original Postcards by:

- Customizing a blank design
- Uploading a complete design
- Using MOO templates if you’re creating Small or Medium Postcards
- If you’re uploading your own custom designs, check the [Artwork Specifications](https://support.moo.com/hc/categories/360002286791) as well as our [Postcard Sizing Templates](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/213487703) before ordering

**Why should I choose Original Postcards?**

Original paper gives your Postcards a premium look and feel for less. You can create matte or glossy Postcards and choose to coat one or both sides. All MOO Postcards are double-sided and full color, and with Printfinity, you can have up to 25 different back designs in your pack.

**How much do Original Postcards cost?**

The cost depends on the size, finish and turnaround time you choose. Use the online [Cost Calculator](https://www.moo.com/us/help/faq/cost-calculator.html) for an accurate quote.

**What are the available finishes for Original Postcards?**

Matte Postcards are 16pt, shine free, have no glare and can be written on. Gloss Postcards are 17pt and have a shiny finish that makes colors more vibrant.

**Do you do any other Postcard paper stocks?**

Yes. MOO also offers Super and Luxe Postcards.

Super is 18–19pt, strong and durable, with a silky Soft Touch coating and optional Gold or Silver Foil.

Luxe is 32pt, extra-thick, uncoated and naturally textured, with a choice of 8 color seams.

**How can I design my Original Postcards?**

You can customize a blank design, upload a complete design or use MOO templates if you’re creating Small or Medium Postcards.

**Do your Original Postcards have any sustainability credentials?**

Original Postcards are made from responsibly sourced FSC® certified material.
