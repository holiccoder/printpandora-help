**What is the minimum order quantity for custom Insulated Tumblers?**The minimum order is **10 tumblers**.**How can I customize the Insulated Tumbler?**You can choose between a **one-sided print,** a **full 360-degree wrap print** or **one-sided engraving** to make it uniquely yours.**What are the dimensions of the Insulated Tumbler?**Each tumbler is **3.35" x 7.28"** (85 x 185mm), designed for easy handling and everyday use.**What colors are the Insulated Tumblers available in?**Our tumblers come in two sleek shades: **Jet Black** and **Cloudy Grey**. Both are versatile and chic.**Can I get a sample?**Absolutely! We offer **MOO-branded samples** that can be shipped within a week. Simply email us at <inquiries@moo.com>.**Are these Insulated Tumblers BPA-free?**Yes, all our drinkware, including these tumblers, is **BPA-free** for safe, worry-free use.**How much does the Insulated Tumbler weigh?**Each tumbler weighs **11.4oz** (324g), striking the perfect balance between lightweight portability and sturdy construction.**Can I put my Insulated Tumbler in the refrigerator, freezer, or microwave?**Feel free to place your tumbler in the refrigerator or freezer. However, it’s **not microwave-safe**, as microwaving can damage the vacuum insulation.**Can I put my Insulated Tumbler in the dishwasher?**No, to preserve their quality, we recommend **hand washing only**.**How long will my beverage stay cold?**Your beverage will stay cold for up to **24 hours**, a whole Earth day.**How long will my beverage stay hot?**Your drink will remain hot for up to **12 hours** (about the length of a day on Saturn), perfect for keeping your beverage warm all day long.**What material is the Insulated Tumbler made of?**Our tumblers are made from **powder-coated stainless steel** with a **clear plastic lid** and **silicone gaskets** to keep everything secure.**Does the lid of the Insulated Tumbler fully close?**The lid is designed to minimize splashes but does **not fully close** at the sip opening.**What’s the capacity of the Insulated Tumbler?**Each tumbler holds **20oz** (568ml), ideal for most beverages.**How will my tumbler be packaged?**Your tumbler will arrive wrapped in a **compostable bag** and placed in an individual **cardboard box** for safe shipping. Orders typically ship in boxes of 10 or 25 tumblers.**How do I return my Insulated Tumbler?**If your tumbler has been **used** or **customized**, it **isn't eligible for a refund**. However, we can assist with recycling it. Please note that we cannot return any product that doesn’t qualify for a refund or exchange.
