**What is this product?**
-------------------------

Despite the name, the [CamelBak Wine Tumbler](https://www.moo.com/us/new/products/camelbak-wine-tumbler/black-one-sided-engraving-12oz) is a versatile, everyday vessel designed for much more than just wine. It is a 12oz insulated stainless steel tumbler perfect for campfires, patios, and office desks. It handles morning coffee just as well as an evening cocktail.

**Is it right for you?**
------------------------

This is a great choice if you are looking for a premium, branded gift that people will actually use. The CamelBak brand signals quality, and the insulation performance (48 hours cold!) is significantly higher than standard tumblers. However, it is a hand-wash-only item and isn't microwave-safe, which is worth considering if you need something low-maintenance for a busy communal kitchen.

#### **Temperature Control**

The standout feature here is the insulation. Keeping drinks cold for 48 hours is exceptional for a tumbler of this size. It also keeps hot beverages at temperature for 12 hours, making it a viable alternative to a standard coffee mug.

#### **The Build**

It comes in a sleek Black finish. The tumbler includes a closable lid, which helps with temperature retention and prevents splashes, though it isn't fully leak-proof if thrown in a bag.

#### **Safety Note**

While it handles hot and cold well, you should use caution with carbonated, perishable, or fermented liquids. These can build up pressure inside the sealed tumbler, which might make the lid pop or spray when opened.

**Care &amp; Safety**
---------------------

This is a high-performance item, and it requires specific care to last.

- **Hand-wash only:** Do not put this in the dishwasher. The high heat and pressure can damage the vacuum seal and the finish.
- **No Microwave:** As a metal product, it is absolutely not microwave-safe.
- **Pressure Warning:** Be careful with fizzy drinks or liquids that can spoil (ferment), as pressure buildup can occur.
