**What is this product?**
-------------------------

The Rite in the Rain Side Spiral Notebook is a compact, weather-resistant notebook designed for writing in challenging conditions. Its rugged cover protects your notes from spills, rain, and mud, making it a dependable option for outdoor work, field notes, and travel.

The notebook also features an impact-resistant spiral binding that keeps its shape even when the notebook is carried in bags, backpacks, or tool kits. With durable construction and specialized pages, it’s built for situations where regular notebooks might fail.

**Is it right for you?**

It’s especially useful for:

- Field work, outdoor projects, or site visits
- Travel notebooks that may be exposed to weather
- Teams that need rugged branded notebooks for events or kits
- Situations where a small, durable notebook is easier to carry than a larger one

If you want a notebook designed to hold up in rain or messy environments, this option is built for exactly that.

**Product details**
-------------------

The Rite in the Rain Side Spiral Notebook is designed for durability in demanding conditions. The cover is made from hard Polydura, a tough material that helps protect notes from scratches, stains, and rough handling.

Inside, the notebook contains white lined pages that provide a clean, organized layout for writing. The compact format makes it easy to carry in pockets, tool bags, or backpacks.

A key feature is the impact-resistant spiral binding. Unlike standard spiral notebooks that can bend or snag, this binding is designed to keep its shape even when the notebook is tossed into a bag or used regularly in the field.

**What to know before ordering**
--------------------------------

Because this notebook is built with specialized paper designed for tough environments, not every type of writing instrument will perform well on it.

Water-based inks may bead up or smear on the paper surface. Choosing the right writing utensil is important to ensure clear, legible notes.

If you plan to customize the notebook, keep in mind that the design must use a single color. Artwork should be prepared accordingly to achieve the best printed result.
