**What is this product?**
-------------------------

The Rocketbook Fusion Letter Notebook Set is a reusable notebook designed for people who want the flexibility of handwritten notes with the convenience of digital storage. It combines traditional writing with cloud-based backup through the Rocketbook app, letting you capture and organize notes digitally while still writing on paper.

The notebook includes multiple page templates such as calendars, to-do lists, and standard note layouts. Because the pages can be wiped clean and reused hundreds of times, it’s designed as a long-lasting alternative to traditional notebooks.

It’s especially useful for students, professionals, and planners who switch between handwritten notes and digital workflows.

**Product details**
-------------------

The Rocketbook Fusion Letter Notebook is designed to blend traditional note-taking with digital organization. Inside the notebook are 42 reusable pages that feature several different templates including calendars, to-do lists, and general note pages.

The notebook uses patented reusable core technology with synthetic pages that allow writing with a special erasable pen. Once your notes have been uploaded or you’re ready to start fresh, the pages can be wiped clean and used again.

This design makes the notebook both durable and practical for everyday use, whether you're managing tasks, planning projects, or capturing ideas.

Each set includes:

- Retail gift box

### **What to know before ordering**

A few practical details to keep in mind before placing your order:

- The notebook requires a Pilot FriXion erasable pen to maintain its reusable functionality. Standard pens will not erase from the pages.
- Writing can remain on the page temporarily, but it’s recommended to wipe pages clean periodically to maintain the best results.
- Because the notebook is designed to be reused, the included microfiber cloth and erasable pen are essential parts of the system.
