It’s dateless, so you’ll never waste a page, and its premium faux-leather cover is beautifully tactile. It’s a mindful, planet-friendly way to get things done.

**What is the minimum order quantity for custom MOO Softcover Planners?**The minimum order quantity is **10 planners**.**How can I customize the MOO Softcover Planner?**You can customize the **front cover** of the MOO Softcover Planner with the following options:

- **Blind Deboss Design:** A subtle, embossed design without color.
- **Metallic Foil Design:** Adds a touch of metallic sheen to your design.

**What are the size and dimensions of the MOO Softcover Planner?**- **Size:** Large/A4
- **Dimensions:** 8.3 x 11.7 inches

**What is the weight of the MOO Softcover Planner?**Each planner weighs **545 grams (19.2 ounces)**.**What color options are available?**- **Jet Black**
- **Midnight Blue**

**How many pages does the MOO Softcover Planner have?**It contains **112 pages**.**What paper does the MOO Softcover Planner use?**- **Type**: Munken Kristall Smooth
- **Weight**: 100gsm

**How does a dateless planner work?**The planner has no pre-assigned dates or years, allowing you to:

- **Start Anytime**: Begin using the planner at any point during the year.
- **Stay Relevant**: Remains useful for **12 months** regardless of the start date.

**What can I use my MOO Softcover Planner for?**You can use it for various purposes, including:

- **Life Planning**
- **Personal Planning**
- **Work Planning**
- **Goal Setting**
- **Productivity Tracking**
- **Nap Scheduling**

**What is the MOO Softcover Planner made of?**- **Cover Material**: Ecorel
    - A recyclable, paper-based imitation leather made from **50% post-consumer waste**.
- **Interior Paper**: Munken Kristall text paper.

**Can I get a sample?****Yes**! To receive a **MOO-branded sample**:

- **Contact**: Email <a rel="noopener">inquiries@moo.com</a>
- **Shipping Time**: Within one week

**Is there a volume discount if I order more planners?****Absolutely**! For volume discounts:

- **Action**: Email <a rel="noopener">inquiries@moo.com</a> with your order details to receive a customized quote.

**Can I mix various colors and finishes in my order?****Unfortunately, no**. Each order must include:

- **One Color**
- **One Finish**

**How are the MOO Softcover Planners packaged?**- **Individual Packaging**: Each planner is placed in an **individual compostable bag**.
- **Order Packaging**: The entire order is **boxed** for secure delivery.
