What are Matte Paper Stickers?
------------------------------

[Matte Paper Stickers](https://www.moo.com/us/new/products/matte-paper-stickers/rectangular-1-x-2-sheet) are made from uncoated paper with a smooth, non-shiny finish. They have a natural, paper-like feel and are designed to be easy to write on.

They’re the most straightforward option in the range. Simple, tactile, and focused on everyday indoor use.


Is this the right choice for you?
---------------------------------

This works well if you’re:

- Labeling products for indoor use
- Writing on stickers (notes, names, dates, packaging details)
- Looking for a recyclable, plastic-free option
- Going for a more natural or understated look

Compared to vinyl stickers, these feel more like traditional paper and are easier to remove. Compared to coated paper stickers, they have no shine and a softer, more tactile finish.

When to consider something else
-------------------------------

You might want a different option if:

- You need full waterproof protection ([vinyl](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036195512) or [coated paper](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/38136760481300) is better)
- Your stickers will be outdoors long-term
- You want a glossy or more polished finish
- You need something highly durable

At a glance
-----------

- **Material**: Uncoated paper
- **Finish**: Matte (non-reflective, smooth feel)
- **Durability**: Wet-strength, suitable for light moisture but not waterproof
- **Sustainability**: 100% recyclable, plastic-free
- **Adhesive**: Easier to remove than vinyl on some surfaces

**Rectangular sizes are available in:**How they compare
----------------

- Compared to [coated paper stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/38136760481300), these are less water-resistant but more natural in feel
- Compared to [vinyl stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036195512), these are less durable but easier to remove and more environmentally friendly
- Compared to [metallic stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/39104152675092), these focus on simplicity rather than visual impact

If you want something minimal and practical, this is usually the right place to start.

Customization: what to expect
-----------------------------

- Printed using water-based ink
- The uncoated surface gives a softer, slightly muted look to colors
- Easy to write on with pen or marker

Because there’s no coating, your design will feel more like printed paper than a sticker with a finish layer.

Common questions
----------------

**Are these waterproof?**  
No. They can handle light moisture but aren’t designed for full water exposure.

**Can I write on them?**  
Yes. The uncoated surface is ideal for writing.

**Are they recyclable?**  
Yes. These are fully recyclable and plastic-free.

**Do they come on rolls?**  
No. They are supplied on sheets.
