What are Coated Paper Stickers?
-------------------------------

[Coated Paper Stickers](https://www.moo.com/us/new/products/coated-paper-stickers/round-1-x-1-sheet) are a paper-based option with a smooth, semi-gloss finish and a protective coating. They’re designed to give you a more polished look than matte paper, while still keeping the feel and benefits of paper.

They’re often chosen when you want something that looks clean and slightly elevated, without moving into full vinyl.

  
Is this the right choice for you?
------------------------------------

This works well if you’re:

- Labeling products that may come into contact with moisture
- Looking for a paper-based option that still feels durable
- Wanting a finish with a bit of shine, but not a glossy plastic look
- Needing something that’s easy to write on

Compared to matte paper stickers, these hold up better to water. Compared to vinyl stickers, they feel more like traditional paper and are recyclable.


When to consider something else
-------------------------------

You might want a different option if:

- You need long-term outdoor durability ([vinyl](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036195512) is better for that)
- You prefer a completely non-shiny, textured feel ([matte paper](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/36861213047828) is a better fit)
- You want a decorative or premium effect like [metallic finishes](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/39104152675092)


At a glance
-----------

- **Material**: Thick paper with semi-gloss coating
- **Finish**: Smooth, semi-gloss (light sheen)
- **Durability**: Waterproof and suitable for short to medium-term outdoor use
- **Adhesive**: Permanent, water-based acrylic
- **Sustainability**: Recyclable, PVC-free

How they compare
----------------

- Compared to [matte paper stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/36861213047828), these are more resistant to water and have a smoother, slightly shinier finish
- Compared to [vinyl stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036195512), these are less durable outdoors but more environmentally friendly
- Compared to [metallic stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/39104152675092), these focus on clarity and readability rather than visual effects

If you want something that sits between paper and vinyl in terms of durability, this is usually the right place.


Customization: what to expect
-----------------------------

- Your design is printed using water-based ink
- The coating helps colors appear a bit more vibrant than uncoated paper
- The surface is still writeable, despite the coating

Because of the semi-gloss layer, designs tend to look a bit sharper than matte paper, especially with darker or more saturated colors.

Common questions
----------------

**Are these waterproof?**  
Yes. The coating makes them resistant to water, though they don’t have the same long-term outdoor protection as vinyl.

**Can I write on them?**  
Yes. You can write on them with most pens or markers.

**Are they recyclable?**  
Yes. These are a recyclable, PVC-free alternative to vinyl.

**Are they easy to remove?**  
No. These use a permanent adhesive and are designed to stay in place.
