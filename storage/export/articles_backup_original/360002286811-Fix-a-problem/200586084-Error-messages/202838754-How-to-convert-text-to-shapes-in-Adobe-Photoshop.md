### What does “convert text to shape” mean?

When you convert text to a shape in Photoshop, the letters are transformed from editable text into vector paths. This allows you to use tools like the **Direct Selection Tool** to move anchor points, reshape letters, and create custom typography effects. After converting, the text is no longer editable as text—you can’t change the font or retype words.

### How do I convert text to a shape in Photoshop?

1. **Create your text**
    
    
    - Select the **Text Tool (T)**.
    - Click on your canvas and type your text.
2. **Select the text layer(s)**
    
    
    - In the **Layers Panel**, click on your text layer(s) to highlight it.
3. **Convert to Shape**
    
    
    - Right-click on the text layer.
    - Choose **Convert to Shape** from the menu.

Your text layer is now a **Shape Layer** and can be edited with shape and path tools.

For the best results, you should then save your PDF, using the preset [**PDF/X-1a:2001**](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/202838774).
