To get the best experience and fastest delivery, it's important to use the version of MOO.com that's tailored to your location.

Check Your Site Version
-----------------------

1. **Look at the top menu bar at the top of the page.** You'll see a flag icon representing your current site version.
2. **If the flag matches your country:** You're all set!
3. **If the flag is incorrect:**
    - Click on the flag icon in the top menu bar.
    - A dropdown menu will appear with available country options.
    - Select your correct country from the list.

Why Selecting the Correct Site Matters
--------------------------------------

We have production centers in both London, UK, and Rhode Island, USA. Choosing the correct site ensures:

- Your products are produced at the closest facility.
- Faster shipping times and lower shipping costs.
- Prices displayed in your local currency.

![Important Icon](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/30843395865364) **Note:** If you're redirected to the incorrect site version, you can manually change it using the steps above.
