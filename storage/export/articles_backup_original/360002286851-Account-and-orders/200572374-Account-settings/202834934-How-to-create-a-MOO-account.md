You can explore our products and even design your own without creating an account. We'll only ask you to set up a MOO account when you want to save a design or place an order. When you decide to save a project or proceed to checkout, you'll be prompted to sign up or log in to MOO.

How to Create a MOO Account
---------------------------

Signing up is easy and only takes a couple of minutes. If you'd like to create an account now, [click here](https://www.moo.com/account/signin.php).

**Using an email address and password?** Select "**I don't have an account**" to get started.

![Create Account Screen](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29833670598036)

*Please note: All orders and re-orders must be placed through your MOO account. We are unable to process payments or orders over the phone.*
