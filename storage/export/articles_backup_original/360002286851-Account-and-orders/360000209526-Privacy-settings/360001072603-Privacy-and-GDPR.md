If you have a MOO account, please note that we do store account and order data. The details of how your personal data is stored, collected, and used can be found in our [Privacy Policy](https://www.moo.com/about/privacy-policy.html). In line with this, you can find a [privacy center](https://www.moo.com/consent/manage) within your MOO account where you can view and make certain decisions about your personal data, including managing your communication preferences.

#### **Unsubscribing from MOO mailings** 

If you do not have a MOO account you can simply click unsubscribe at the bottom of any email you've received or contact our Customer Service team to unsubscribe from physical mailings. If you do have a MOO account you can manage your mailing preferences from within your account's [privacy center](https://www.moo.com/consent/manage).

Please note that it is not possible to unsubscribe from our privacy policy emails. For legal reasons, we must notify our subscribers anytime our privacy policy has changed.

#### **Right to erasure** 

If you wish to exercise your right to erasure (also known as the ‘right to be forgotten’) and have your MOO data removed, please email our data privacy manager directly at <data.privacymanager@moo.com>.

#### **Right of access**

If you wish to exercise your right of access, have your data sent back to you or if you wish to send us your data processing addendum then please contact our data privacy manager at <data.privacymanager@moo.com>.
