From 1st July 2018, the Australian government requires us to charge 10% GST to consumers and non-GST registered businesses for orders less than AU$1,000. GST will automatically be added to your basket.

Please note, if your order is over AU$1,000 and being shipped to Australia you will NOT pay the GST directly on moo.com but instead, you will have to pay the 10% GST rate at customs.
