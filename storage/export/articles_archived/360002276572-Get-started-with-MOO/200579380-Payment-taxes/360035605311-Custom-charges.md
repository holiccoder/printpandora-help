All products are shipped from our US warehouse and therefore any orders being shipped internationally may incur customs charges. A customs charge or tariff may be assessed when goods are transported across international borders, depending on the weight and value of the parcel as well as local guidelines. Due to this, please note that MOO cannot take responsibility for these charges.

In addition, we cannot confirm whether a customs fee or tariff will be charged or not at the time of placing an order, as this will depend on whether the parcel is selected for inspection by border control.

For orders shipping to Canada, all handling charges including taxes, customs, and courier fees will be assessed and paid for when checking out on the MOO website, so no customs charges should be required at the point of delivery.
