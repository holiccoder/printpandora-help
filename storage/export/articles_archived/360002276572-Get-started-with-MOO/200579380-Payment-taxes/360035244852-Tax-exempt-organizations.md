For eligible tax-exempt organizations or persons, we will gladly refund the tax charged if you are shipping to one of the following [states](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/360020721812).

We do, however, request a copy of the state-issued exempt form to be emailed to our finance team once your order is placed and then again each time you place a new order. You can send these forms over to \*<salestax-us@moo.com> along with your order number.

We will require for these forms to be emailed over for every new order so that they can verify and process your request, ensuring we have up to date forms.

![icon__ui--warning-circle-bold-red.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/360078609791/icon__ui--warning-circle-bold-red.svg) **\* If you have a MOO Business Account, please email your [Account Manager](https://www.moo.com/mbs/help) directly with your forms and order number and they will take care of the rest for you.**
