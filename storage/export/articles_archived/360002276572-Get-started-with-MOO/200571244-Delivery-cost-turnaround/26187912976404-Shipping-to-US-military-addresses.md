Orders shipping to US military and diplomatic addresses (including APO/FPO/DPO) can only be shipped with USPS. For this reason, they must be placed on our US site (moo.com/us) with United States set as the destination country and the slowest shipping option must be selected.

Please note delivery times will be longer than reflected in the cart if the destination is not within the continental USA.

Details on how to format US military and diplomatic addresses can be found on USPS' site:  
<https://www.usps.com/ship/apo-fpo-dpo.htm>
