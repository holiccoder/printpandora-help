 **Customization**
-----------------

Make it yours. The Karst Softcover Notebook can be customized with your branding, so every time someone opens it, they know where it came from.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/49249236839572)**Single Color Printing** lets you apply a one-color design directly to the cover.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/49249252528532)**Blind Debossing** presses your design into the cover material, creating a tactile, embossed effect with no ink.

**What can be customized:** Your logo, artwork, or design can be applied to the cover.   
**Single color printing** works best with clean, high-contrast artwork.   
**Blind debossing** is ideal for logos and simple shapes that benefit from texture and depth.

**The process:** Choose your decoration method, upload your artwork through MOO's online design tool and preview before you order.

**Artwork guidance:** For best results, submit artwork as a high-resolution file at 300dpi. Vector files are preferred for logos.

**Care &amp; handling**
-----------------------

Stone paper is non-porous, so ink takes slightly longer to dry than it would on regular paper. Give it a moment before closing the notebook, especially if you write quickly or use wetter inks. Fountain pens work with this paper, just allow a little extra drying time.

###  

**Sustainability**
------------------

The pages are made from sustainably recycled stone, with no bleaches or acids used in production. The notebook holds a **Cradle to Cradle (C2C) Silver Certification**, which means it's recyclable at end of life. A notebook you can feel good about using, and good about disposing of responsibly.

**Frequently asked questions**
------------------------------

**What's the minimum order?**   
The minimum order quantity is 20 notebooks.

**Can I use a fountain pen in this notebook?**   
Yes, you can use any writing utensil. Fountain pens just need a little extra drying time since stone paper is non-porous and ink sits on the surface rather than absorbing quickly.

**Will ink bleed through the pages?**   
No. Because stone paper is non-absorbent, ink doesn't bleed through to the other side.

**Is this notebook recyclable?**   
Yes. The Karst notebook holds a Cradle to Cradle Silver Certification, so it can be recycled when you're done with it.
