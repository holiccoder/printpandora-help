![Perpetual Planner Image 2](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/25884470155924)It's a planner without any dates or years. That means you can buy it at any time in the year (and don't have to get a new one if you haven't finished it in 12 months). It's a more flexible and sustainable way of getting things done.**What's a Perpetual Planner? How does a dateless planner work?****What is the minimum order quantity for custom planners?**You can order a minimum of **10 planners**. Find all the details and place your order [here](https://www.moo.com/us/planners/custom). Download sizing templates [here](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/25884629378964).**What are the dimensions of the planners?**Each planner measures **5.12" x 8.15"** (130mm x 207mm) and includes **152 pages**.**How are the planners made?**Crafted with **Swiss binding**, our planners lay flat on any page, making writing effortless.**What is the Calendar Clip?**True to its name, the Calendar Clip clips to the **top of each page**. It marks where you’re at in the planner – and **it lets you know what week you’re on**. Just slide it to match where the days fall.**What colors is the Perpetual Planner available in?**The planner comes in four stylish color combinations:

- **Jet Black** with Duck Egg accents
- **Charcoal Grey** with Mint accents
- **Midnight Blue** with Tan accents
- **Berry Red** with Turquoise accents

 **Can I mix the planner colors and finishes within my order?**Currently, you can choose only **one color and finish per order**.**How many pages are in the planner?**The planner includes a total of **152 pages**:

- Four sections of three months of weekly spreads (32 pages each)
- Three colored sections with cross/dot grid pages (8 pages each)

**Can I remove the MOO logo from the back of the planner?**The MOO logo cannot be removed through the standard ordering process. However, we offer bespoke printing for larger quantities. For more information, please email our Sales team at [**inquiries@moo.com**](mailto:inquiries@moo.com).**What is the page weight?**The monthly pages are **104gsm**, and the divider pages are **135gsm**.**Can I get a sample?**Yes! We can ship you a **MOO-branded sample** within a week. Just email us at [**inquiries@moo.com**](mailto:inquiries@moo.com) to request one.
