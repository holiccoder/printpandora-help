**What is this product?**
-------------------------

The **Rite in the Rain Top Spiral Notebook** is a compact, weather-resistant notebook designed to protect your notes in tough conditions. Its rugged cover helps shield pages from rain, spills, mud, and everyday wear, making it a reliable option for outdoor work, field notes, or travel.

The notebook also features an impact-resistant spiral binding that keeps its shape even when carried in a bag or used in demanding environments. It’s a practical choice for professionals, students, and anyone who needs a notebook that can handle unpredictable weather.

**Product details**
-------------------

The Rite in the Rain Top Spiral Notebook is built for durability and reliability in unpredictable environments. The cover is made from **hard Polydura**, a tough material that helps protect the notebook from scratches, stains, and harsh weather.

Inside, the notebook contains **50 white lined pages**, providing a simple, structured layout for writing notes, lists, or observations. The **spiral binding is impact-resistant**, meaning it holds its shape even if the notebook is frequently carried in bags, toolkits, or outdoor gear.

Its compact size options make it easy to carry while still offering enough space for everyday notes.


### **What to know before ordering**

Because this notebook is designed for outdoor use, the specialized paper works best with certain writing tools.

Water-based inks do not perform well on this type of paper. Gel pens, fountain pens, and highlighters may smear or wash away if the pages get wet. Choosing the right writing instrument will help ensure clear, readable notes.

If you plan to customize the notebook, keep in mind that **designs are printed in a single color**, so artwork should be prepared accordingly.

**Care &amp; Safety**
---------------------

When using the notebook:

- **When dry:** Permanent markers or standard ballpoint pens can be used.
- **When wet or dry:** Pencils, Rite in the Rain pens, wax markers, crayons, or oil paint work well.

Gel pens, fountain pens, and highlighters are not recommended. Water-based inks may bead up, smear, or wash off the specialized paper.
