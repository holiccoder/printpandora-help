**Customization**
-----------------

Your notebook, your brand. You can add your logo or artwork in two ways:

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/49248789613972)**Single Color Printing** places your design on the cover in one color.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/49248757195668)**Blind Debossing** presses your artwork into the cover without ink, creating a subtle, tactile impression that's understated in the best possible way.

**What you can customize:** Logo, artwork, or text on the cover. One color for printed designs, or a debossed impression for a premium feel.

**The process:** Upload your artwork through MOO's platform. High-resolution files at 300dpi work best, and vector files are ideal for logos and sharp graphics.

**Care &amp; handling**
-----------------------

The stone pages are water-repellant and tear-resistant, so normal use won't faze them. Ink won't bleed through, but because the surface is non-porous, let the ink dry before closing the notebook. Standard ballpoint and gel pens, and pencils, work great. Fountain pens need a little extra drying time.

###  

**Sustainability**
------------------

The stone pages are made from sustainably recycled stone, produced without bleaches or acids. The notebook carries a **Cradle to Cradle (C2C) Silver Certification**, which means it can be recycled at the end of its life.

**Frequently asked questions**
------------------------------

**What's the difference between Single Color Printing and Blind Debossing?**   
**Single Color Printing** adds your design in ink on the cover.   
**Blind Debossing** presses your design into the cover material without color, giving it a subtle, textured look.

**What's the minimum order?**   
The minimum is **16 notebooks** per order.

**Will ink bleed through the stone pages?**   
No. The non-porous surface means ink doesn't bleed through. It can take a moment longer to dry than on regular paper, especially with wetter inks, so let it set before you close the notebook.

**Is the notebook recyclable?**   
Yes. The Karst notebook holds a Cradle to Cradle Silver Certification, which means it meets established standards for recyclability.
