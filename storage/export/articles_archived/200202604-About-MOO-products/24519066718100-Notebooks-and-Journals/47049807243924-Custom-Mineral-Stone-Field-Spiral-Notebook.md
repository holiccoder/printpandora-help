**What is this product?**
-------------------------

The Mineral Stone Field Spiral Notebook is a durable, eco-friendly notebook designed for everyday note-taking, field work, and on-the-go use. Instead of traditional paper, its pages are made from crushed stone and resin, which means no trees are used in the paper production.

Because of this unique material, the pages are naturally water-repellent, tear-resistant, and smooth to write on. It’s a practical option for outdoor work, travel, or anyone who wants a notebook that can handle a little wear and tear without falling apart.

**Product details**
-------------------

The Mineral Stone Field Spiral Notebook combines durable construction with environmentally conscious materials.

The cover is made from recycled polypropylene, giving the notebook a sturdy outer layer that helps protect the pages inside. The pages themselves are made from crushed stone combined with a non-toxic resin. This creates smooth writing pages that resist tearing and repel water.

The result is a notebook that can hold up to daily use, whether you’re writing at your desk, in a workshop, or outdoors. The spiral binding allows the notebook to lie flat for easy writing, while the compact 7" × 5" size makes it easy to carry in a bag or backpack.
