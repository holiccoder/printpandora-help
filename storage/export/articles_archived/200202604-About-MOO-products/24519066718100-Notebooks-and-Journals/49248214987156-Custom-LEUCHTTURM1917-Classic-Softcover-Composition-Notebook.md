**Customization**
-----------------

Make it yours. The LEUCHTTURM1917 Classic Softcover Composition is available with two decoration options:

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/49248199024020)**Single Color Printing** puts your logo or artwork directly on the cover in one color.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/49248214985876)**Blind Debossing** presses your design into the faux leather cover, creating an elegant tactile impression with no ink.

**What can be customized:** The cover. Use your logo, wordmark or custom artwork to make it yours.

**The process:** Upload your artwork through MOO's online tool and place your order. Templates are available to help you get the placement right.

**Artwork guidance:** For best results, supply high-resolution artwork at 300dpi. For debossing especially, vector files with clean, well-defined edges work best.

**Frequently asked questions**

**What's the minimum order for a custom LEUCHTTURM1917 notebook?**
