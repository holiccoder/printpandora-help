With MOO's Custom Softcover Journals, choose from 8 vibrant color combinations, personalize the cover with debossing or one of four foil options, and select between lined or dotted pages.

**What is the minimum order quantity for Custom Journals?**You can order a minimum of **25 Journals**. Find all the details and place your order [here](https://www.moo.com/us/notebooks/custom). Download templates [here](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/24555208800276).**What are the dimensions of the Journal?**Each Journal measures **5.12" x 8.15"** (130mm x 207mm) and includes **64 pages**.**What colors are the Journals available in?**Our Journals come in eight stylish colors:

- **Black** with Grey central pages
- **Grey** with Grey central pages
- **Mint Green** with Grey central pages
- **Steel Blue** with Grey central pages
- **Tan** with Grey central pages
- **Pastel Pink** with Grey central pages
- **Lavender** with Grey central pages
- **Turquoise** with Grey central pages

**How can I customize the Journal?**Personalize the **front cover** with your choice of **Blind Deboss**, or **Gold**, **Silver**, **Black**, or **White Foil**.![zxqcdszvzb6qzkjp9vgi.jpg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/30838733966740)
