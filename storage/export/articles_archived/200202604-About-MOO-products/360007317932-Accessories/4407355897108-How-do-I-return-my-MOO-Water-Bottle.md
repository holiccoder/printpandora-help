If you're not completely satisfied with your MOO Water Bottle, you can return it to us within **30 days** of receiving it for a full refund. Please make sure to:

- Return the bottle in its **original packaging**.
- Ensure the bottle is **unused**.
- Include your **order reference number**.

Return Address
--------------

Please send your return to the following address:

MOO  
25 Fairmount Ave  
East Providence, RI 02914  
United States Returning After 30 Days
-----------------------

If you're returning your bottle **after 30 days** of receiving it, please contact our customer service team before sending it back:

Email: <support@moo.com>

In your email, please include:

- Your contact details and order number.
- The reason for your return.
- Any relevant photos of the product.

Our team will get back to you within 24 hours of receiving your email.

**Important Notes:**

- If the bottle has been **used**, it won’t be eligible for a refund.
- MOO is not responsible for shipping back any product deemed ineligible for a refund or exchange.
