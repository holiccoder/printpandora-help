![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48452801515156)![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48452766088724)What are MiniCards?
-------------------

[MiniCards](https://www.moo.com/business-cards/minicards) are compact business cards designed for short, focused designs. They’re smaller than standard business cards, which gives them a more distinctive look and makes them useful for quick contact details, tags, mini promos, or simple brand moments.

They’re printed double-sided in full color and come in one small format: **2.75" x 1.10"**.

  
Is this right for you?
-------------------------

This works well if you want:

- A smaller card that stands out from standard sizes
- A simple layout with just the essentials
- Something that can work as a mini business card, hang tag, or tiny bookmark

Compared to standard Business Cards:

- Smaller and more minimal in layout
- Less space for detailed information
- Better suited to concise designs and short text
- Available in fewer paper options than standard Business Cards

  
At a glance
--------------

- **Size:** 2.75" x 1.10" / 70 x 28mm
- **Paper options:**
    
    ![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48452766092308)**Original**
    
    ![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48452801532436)**Luxe**
- **Original finishes:** Matte or Gloss
- **Luxe finish:** Uncoated with a choice of [8 color seams](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/202670074)
- **Printing:** Full color on both sides
- **Best for:** Minimal designs, quick contact sharing, tags, and promotional inserts

  
Size and format
------------------

MiniCards are a single, compact size:

**1.1" x 2.75"**

You can choose:

- **Square corners**
- **Rounded corners** (Original paper only)

Because the format is much smaller than a standard business card, it’s best to keep the design simple and avoid cramming in too much information.

  
Paper options
----------------

### Original MiniCards

Original MiniCards are printed on MOO’s classic **16pt** paper stock.

- Available with **matte or gloss** finish

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48452766095252)**Matte finish**

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48452801537684)**Gloss finish**

- Available with square or rounded corners
- Coated on both sides
- Smooth surface that works well for colorful designs and photography
- Easier choice if you want a more traditional printed finish

### Luxe MiniCards

Luxe MiniCards are made from **32pt** Mohawk Superfine® paper.

- Built from **four layers** of paper
- Only available with square corners
- **Uncoated**, so you can write on both sides
- Available with a choice of **8 color seams**
- Better if you want something thicker, more tactile, or more distinctive

  
What makes MiniCards different?
----------------------------------

The main difference is the size.

Because MiniCards are so compact, they work best when the design is focused on just a few elements, like:

- A name
- A logo
- A website or handle
- A short message

MiniCards are also useful beyond traditional networking cards, including as:

- **Hang tags**
- **Tiny bookmarks**
- Small promotional pieces

Customization
-------------

- Full-color printing is available on both sides
- Original gives you a choice of **matte or gloss**
- Luxe gives you an **uncoated finish** and [8 seam color options](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/202670074)

![Printfinity.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48452766100884)While the front of every card in your pack will have the same image, you can have up to 100 different back designs when utilizing MOO’s own [Printfinity](https://www.moo.com/about/printfinity) option! *Please note, Printfinity options may be limited on some MOO Designs.*  
What to know before ordering
-------------------------------

- There’s less room for text than on a standard business card
- Small text can become hard to read, so simpler layouts work better
- Original is better for bright, coated print finishes
- Luxe is better if you want extra thickness and a more tactile feel
- If you’re uploading your own artwork, MOO provides MiniCard templates and artwork guidelines on the product page
