![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48453617782164)![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48453617785236)What are Boxed Luxe Business Cards?
-----------------------------------

[Boxed Luxe Business Cards](https://www.moo.com/us/new/products/boxed-luxe-business-cards/us-white) combine extra-thick Luxe cards with a premium presentation box. Instead of receiving loose cards, your order comes neatly packaged in a display-ready box designed to protect and showcase them.

The cards themselves are made from layered Mohawk Superfine® paper, while the box is crafted from high-quality Japanese paper.

  
Is this right for you?
-------------------------

This works well if you want:

- A premium, gift-ready presentation
- Extra-thick business cards with a strong first impression
- A clean, minimal look with elevated packaging

Compared to standard Luxe Business Cards:

- Includes a presentation box instead of loose packaging
- Fewer customization options (no Printfinity, limited seam colors)
- More focused on presentation and simplicity

  
At a glance
--------------

- **Card paper:** Luxe (600gsm / 32pt)
- **Card feel:** Extra-thick, rigid, naturally textured
- **Seam options:** Raven Black or Polar White
- **Box material:** Premium Japanese paper (50% recycled)
- **Packaging:** Plastic-free, compostable bag
- **Printfinity:** Not available
- **Material:** Made from responsibly sourced [FSC® certified material](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/38741101861652)
- **Best for:** Premium presentation, gifting, standout branding

Paper and construction
-------------------------

Luxe cards are made using a layered construction that fuses four sheets of Mohawk Superfine® paper together.

This creates:

- A **600gsm / 32pt thickness** (about as thick as a credit card)
- A **rigid, durable feel**
- A **naturally textured, uncoated surface**

The uncoated finish also means you can write on either side of the card.

The presentation box
-----------------------

Each set of cards comes in a dedicated display box.

- Made from **premium Japanese paper**
- Contains **50% recycled material**
- **Plastic-free** and packaged in a compostable bag
- Designed to store and present your cards cleanly

Customization
----------------

- Full-color printing available on both sides of the cards
- Uncoated surface gives a more **natural, slightly muted print finish**
- Seam color adds contrast without additional finishes

Printfinity is **not available** for this product.

  
What to know before ordering
-------------------------------

- Minimum order is **1 box (50 cards)**
- Cards and box are **not customizable beyond listed options**
- Designed for simplicity and presentation rather than flexibility
- The seam color and box are fixed choices depending on selection

If you’re uploading your own artwork, be sure to check the [artwork specifications](https://support.moo.com/hc/categories/360002286791) before placing your order.
