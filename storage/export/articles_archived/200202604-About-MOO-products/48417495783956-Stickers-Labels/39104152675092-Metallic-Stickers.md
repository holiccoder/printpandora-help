What are Metallic Stickers?
---------------------------

[Metallic stickers](https://www.moo.com/us/new/products/metallic-stickers/round-1-x-1-gold-sheet) are paper-based stickers with a brushed aluminum layer that gives your design a subtle metallic shine. They’re available in gold and silver, with a smooth, matte-like finish that reflects light without being overly glossy.

They’re less about durability and more about visual impact.

Is this the right choice for you?
---------------------------------

This works well if you’re:

- Adding a premium or decorative touch to packaging
- Creating designs that play with metallic highlights
- Looking for something that stands out without being overly shiny
- Using stickers in indoor or low-exposure environments

Compared to vinyl or coated paper stickers, these are more about appearance than performance. Compared to matte paper, they feel more elevated and eye-catching.

When to consider something else
-------------------------------

You might want a different option if:

- You need waterproof or long-term outdoor durability ([vinyl](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036195512) or [coated paper](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/38136760481300) is better)
- You want a fully recyclable option ([matte](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/36861213047828) or [coated paper](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/38136760481300) is better suited)
- You need a completely flat, non-reflective finish

At a glance
-----------

- **Material**: Paper-based with brushed aluminum layer
- **Finish**: Metallic (subtle matte shine)
- **Colors**: Gold and Silver
- **Durability**: Wet-strength, suitable for light moisture but not outdoor use
- **Adhesive**: Permanent, biobased adhesive

----------------

- Compared to [coated paper stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/38136760481300), these are less durable but more visually distinctive
- Compared to [vinyl stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036195512), these are not designed for outdoor use but offer a more decorative finish
- Compared to [clear stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/46009040781588), these add visual weight instead of blending into the surface

If your priority is how the sticker looks rather than how long it lasts, this is the right direction.

Customization: what to expect
-----------------------------

Metallic stickers behave a bit differently from other types because the material itself is part of the design:

- Transparent areas in your artwork reveal the metallic surface underneath
- White areas print as white ink, covering the metallic effect
- Designs need to be set up intentionally to take advantage of the metallic finish

This means your artwork choices directly control where the metallic effect appears.

Common questions
----------------

**Are these waterproof?**  
No. They can handle some moisture but aren’t designed for outdoor use.

**Can I write on them?**  
Yes, with permanent markers or some pens. If you need something easier to write on, matte or coated paper may be better.

**Do they fade in sunlight?**  
They’re not UV-protected, so they’re not recommended for outdoor exposure.

**Are they recyclable?**  
No. If recyclability is important, matte or coated paper stickers are better options.
