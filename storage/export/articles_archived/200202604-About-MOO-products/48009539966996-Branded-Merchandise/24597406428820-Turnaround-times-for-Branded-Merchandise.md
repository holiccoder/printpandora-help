**Please note that our Branded Merchandise products do take longer to produce than our paper-printed products.**

Depending on the customized product it can take anywhere from 5-11 business days to personalize and dispatch.

If you are in a rush for your order be sure to select the fastest shipping method at checkout. Once the order is placed we will not be able to change the shipping method.

**Please note that this article refers to our Branded Merchandise products.**

**For our paper-printed products, please find product information and guidance** [**here**](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us) **and support** [**here**](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/204288414)**.**
