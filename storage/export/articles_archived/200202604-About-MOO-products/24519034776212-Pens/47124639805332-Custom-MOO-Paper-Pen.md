**What is this product?**
-------------------------

The **MOO Paper Pen** is a ballpoint pen made primarily from sturdy paper, designed as a more planet-friendly alternative to traditional plastic pens. It’s a simple, practical writing tool that stands out because of its unusual material and minimalist design.

These pens are especially popular for **event giveaways, conferences, and trade shows**, where brands want something useful that also sparks conversation. Because they’re made from paper and packaged in compostable bags, they offer a thoughtful option for businesses looking to reduce reliance on single-use plastics.

**Is it right for you?**
------------------------

The MOO Paper Pen is a good fit if you are looking for:

- An **affordable branded giveaway** for events or conferences
- A **conversation-starting promotional item** made from unexpected materials
- A **more environmentally considerate alternative** to plastic pens
- A **simple everyday ballpoint pen** for note taking or quick writing

It may not be the best choice if you are looking for a **refillable pen**. If that’s important, the **MOO Twist Pen** or **MOO Slim Pen** may be better options.

**Product details**
-------------------

The MOO Paper Pen is built around a **rolled paper barrel**, creating a lightweight but sturdy writing instrument. Inside the paper body is a standard **ballpoint ink cartridge**, providing smooth black ink for everyday writing tasks.

Its clean design makes it a strong choice for **brand giveaways**. The paper surface provides a distinctive look and feel compared with traditional plastic pens, which can make it more memorable when handed out at events or meetings.

Each pen writes with **black ballpoint ink**, suitable for general office use, note taking, or signing documents.

**Customization**
-----------------

Paper Pens can be personalized using **laser engraving**.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/47124639801876)**Laser Engraving**

Laser engraving works by using a focused beam of light to **etch your design directly into the pen’s surface**. Instead of adding ink or paint, the process removes a thin layer of material to create the design. The result is a **clean, permanent mark** that won’t rub off during normal use.

This method is well suited to **logos, brand names, or simple graphics**, especially for promotional giveaways where durability matters.
