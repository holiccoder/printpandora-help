**Product details**
-------------------

The Recycled Stainless Steel Ballpoint Pen combines a modern metal look with environmentally conscious materials. The **stainless steel barrel made from recycled material** gives the pen a premium weight and durability compared to plastic alternatives.

The pen uses **ballpoint ink with a 1.0 mm tip**, providing a smooth writing experience suitable for everyday tasks such as note-taking, form filling, and signatures. Each cartridge offers approximately **800 meters of writing length** before needing replacement.

Because the ink cartridge can be replaced, the pen can continue to be used long after the original ink runs out.

**Customization**
-----------------

### **How you can make it yours**

This pen can be customized with **one-sided engraving**.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/47047762774804)Engraving uses a precision laser to etch your design directly into the stainless steel barrel. Instead of adding ink or paint, the engraving **removes a thin layer of metal**, leaving a permanent mark that won’t fade or wear off easily.

This method works best for:

- Logos
- Company names
- Short messages or taglines

Because engraving does not use colored ink, the final result appears as a **clean metallic etch against the silver surface**.
