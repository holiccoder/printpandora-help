**Customization**
-----------------

### **How you can make it yours**

This pen can be customized with **one-sided engraving**.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/47048680156564)Engraving removes a thin layer of the metal surface to reveal the design underneath. The result is a **precise, permanent mark** that won't fade, peel, or rub off over time.

Because engraving does not use ink, designs typically work best when they:

- Use **clean lines**
- Avoid extremely small details
- Are optimized for a **single placement area**

The engraved finish complements the pen’s minimalist design and gives branding a professional, long-lasting appearance.
