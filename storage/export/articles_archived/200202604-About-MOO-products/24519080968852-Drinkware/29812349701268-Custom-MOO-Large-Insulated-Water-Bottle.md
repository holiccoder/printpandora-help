**What is the minimum order quantity for custom Insulated Water Bottles?**You can order a minimum of **10 bottles**.**How can I customize the Insulated Water Bottles?**Choose between a **one-sided print**, a **full 360-degree wrap print** or **one-sided engraving** for maximum creative flexibility in displaying your brand.**What are the dimensions of the Insulated Water Bottles?**- **Large:** 3.54” x 10.63” (90 x 270mm)
- **XL:** 3.54” x 12.01” (90 x 305mm)

These sizes are perfect for **everyday use**, offering **generous capacity** while remaining **easy to carry**.

**What colors are the Insulated Water Bottles available in?**Our bottles come in two sleek shades: **Jet Black** and **Cloudy Grey**. Both colors are **versatile** and **stylish**.**Can I get a sample?**Yes! **MOO-branded samples** are available and can be shipped within a week. Just email us at [**inquiries@moo.com**](mailto:inquiries@moo.com) to request one.**Are these water bottles BPA free?**Absolutely, all MOO water bottles are **BPA-free**, ensuring **safe and healthy drinking**.**How much do the Insulated Water Bottles weigh?**- **Large:** 14.8 oz (420g)
- **XL:** 1.1 lb (484g)

These weights make the bottles **light enough for on-the-go use** while **sturdy enough for any setting**.

**Can I put my Insulated Water Bottle in the refrigerator, freezer, or microwave?**You can safely store your bottle in the **refrigerator** or **freezer**. However, it’s **not microwave-safe**; microwaving could **damage the vacuum insulation**.**Can I put my Insulated Water Bottle in the dishwasher?**No, to maintain their quality, we recommend **hand washing only**.**How long will my beverage stay cold?**Your beverage will stay cold for up to **24 hours**, a whole Earth day.**How long will my beverage stay hot?**Your drink will remain hot for up to **12 hours**, perfect for keeping your beverage warm throughout the day.**What material is the Insulated Water Bottle made of?**The bottle is made from **powder-coated stainless steel**, and the cap is crafted from **durable black plastic** with **silicone gaskets** to **prevent leaks** and keep it secure.**What’s the capacity of the Insulated Water Bottle?**- **Large:** 32 oz
- **XL:** 40 oz

These sizes provide **ample space for hydration** or your hot beverage of choice, whether you need to **stay hydrated** or **keep your coffee warm**.

**How will my bottle be packaged?**Each bottle is wrapped in a **compostable bag** and placed in an **individual cardboard box** for safe shipping.
