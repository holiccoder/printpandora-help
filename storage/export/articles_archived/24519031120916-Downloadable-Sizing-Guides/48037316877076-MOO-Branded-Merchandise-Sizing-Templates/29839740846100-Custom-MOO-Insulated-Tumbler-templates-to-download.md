Create your artwork in **CMYK**, using vector files where possible or raster images at **300 dpi** or higher. Make sure lines are at least **0.5 pt** thick, avoid **transparency**, and keep font sizes above **6 pt** with all fonts **outlined**. Set your artwork to **RGB Black (0/0/0)** and ensure all fonts are **outlined** and at least **6 pt** in size. Avoid sharp corners, solid areas with knockout text, and vertical lines longer than **1.00″**, and make sure all lines are at least **0.65 pt** thick.
