Make sure all lines are at least **0.75 pt** thick and use a minimum font size of **8 pt**, taking extra care with serif fonts. Submit your artwork as a **vector file in 100% black**, then outline all fonts, remove the **info layer**, and export your file as a **PDF/X-1a**.\* may appear blank until opened in Adobe software. Please do not save your design in the 'artwork guidelines' group layer or it will not print.
