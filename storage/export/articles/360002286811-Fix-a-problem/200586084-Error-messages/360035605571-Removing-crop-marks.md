At {{BRAND_NAME}}, we use a bleed and safe area system for printing, which means you do **not** need to include crop marks in your design files. Our specifications help us identify where to trim your products accurately.

Before You Upload Your Design
-----------------------------

- **Remove any crop marks** from your files.
- Ensure your design fits within our [bleed and safe area guidelines](https://{{HELP_DOMAIN}}/hc/sections/200572464).

If your files contain crop marks, please remove them before uploading. Otherwise, they may appear on your final products.
