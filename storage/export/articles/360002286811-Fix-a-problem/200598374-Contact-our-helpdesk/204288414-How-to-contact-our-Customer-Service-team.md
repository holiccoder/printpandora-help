- **When:** Mon – Fri, 4:00 AM – 7:00 PM (EDT)  
    *(Excluding Public Holidays)*
- **Ideal for:** Quick questions and order support

####  

- **Response Time:** We'll reply by the end of the next working day, often much sooner
- **Best for:** Technical or artwork issues
