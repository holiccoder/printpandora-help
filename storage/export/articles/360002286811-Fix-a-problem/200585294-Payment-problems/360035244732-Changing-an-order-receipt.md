After placing an order, you will have access to an order receipt that will live in your account under [Order History](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/us/account/order-history.php) for access at any time.

- Click on the order number you wish to download the receipt of.
- Click the **Download a receipt** link in the **Payment details** box.

If you need changes made to this receipt after an order has been placed, please contact our [Customer Service](http://{{CONTACT_URL}}) team.
