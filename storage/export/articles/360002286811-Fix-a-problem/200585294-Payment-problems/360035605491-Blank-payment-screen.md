Some ad-blocking applications, anti-virus software, and certain network restrictions can keep the payment fields from appearing at checkout. You might be seeing the same as the image below:

![no_payment_fields.png]({{IMAGE_PENDING:29834216562708}})

To fix this we recommend trying the following:

- Temporarily disabling any ad-blocking extension or software.
- Checking out in an incognito/private browsing window. 
    - **Chrome**: Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + N
    - **Firefox**: Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + P
    - **Safari**: Cmd + Shift + N
- Switching browsers
- Checking out as a Guest using Paypal.
- If you are using a VPN, temporarily disable it.

This may also happen if there is a formatting error in the billing or shipping address, especially if your browser auto-filled some of the fields. To resolve this, we recommend going back to re-enter your billing and shipping address, making sure that:

- Each part of the address is in the correct field (a company name is only needed if it is a business address)
- There are no typos or extra spaces
- The correct country has been selected
- The postcode has been entered
- A phone number has been provided
- The shipping address is not a PO Box

If, after following the above steps, the payment screen remains blank, please contact our [Customer Service](http://{{CONTACT_URL}}) team to investigate further.
