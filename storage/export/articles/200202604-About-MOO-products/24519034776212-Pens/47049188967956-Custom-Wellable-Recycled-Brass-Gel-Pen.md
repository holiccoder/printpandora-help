**Customization**
-----------------

### **How you can make it yours**

This pen can be personalized using **one-sided engraving or one-sided printing**.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/47112106186260)**One-sided engraving**

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/47049158250132)**One-sided printing**

**Engraving** removes a thin layer of the surface to reveal the brass underneath. The result is a clean, durable design that won’t wear off over time. This option is often chosen for a refined, professional look.

**Printing** applies your artwork directly onto the pen’s surface. This approach can make logos or graphics more visually prominent depending on the design.

When preparing artwork, it’s important to design with the single decoration area in mind so your logo remains clear and readable.
