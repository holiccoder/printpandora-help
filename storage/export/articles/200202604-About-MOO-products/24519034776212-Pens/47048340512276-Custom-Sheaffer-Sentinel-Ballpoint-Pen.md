### **How you can make it yours**

The pen can be customized using **one-sided engraving**.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/47048327419540)Engraving works by precisely etching your design into the pen’s surface. This method produces a **clean, permanent mark that will not fade or wear off**, making it ideal for logos, names, or short text.

Because engraving is typically **single-color and detail-based**, simple designs and clear linework tend to produce the best results. Placement is on one side of the pen to ensure the branding remains visible during use.
