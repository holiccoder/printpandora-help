**Product details**
-------------------

The MOO Slim Pen combines a minimalist silhouette with durable construction. Its lacquered metal body provides a smooth finish and a solid feel while remaining lightweight enough for comfortable everyday writing.

The pen uses **Parker-style ballpoint ink** with a **medium 1.0 mm tip**, offering consistent ink flow for note-taking, signing documents, and everyday writing tasks.

The twist mechanism engages the writing tip smoothly and keeps the design clean without the need for a click button. Because the pen is refillable using **Schmidt G2 standard size cartridges**, it can be reused again and again.


### **What to know before ordering**

A few practical things to keep in mind when ordering Slim Pens:

- Customization is applied through **one-sided engraving**, creating a clean, permanent finish on the metal barrel.
- Engraving works best with simple logos, text, or line-based artwork.
- Customized pens are **not eligible for refunds or exchanges**.
- Each pen is packaged individually in a **compostable bag** rather than a gift box.

**Customization**
-----------------

The Slim Pen can be personalized using **one-sided engraving**.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/47124325095828)**One-sided engraving**

Engraving removes a thin layer of the pen’s surface to reveal your design permanently in the metal body. This method creates a clean, durable mark that won’t fade, peel, or rub off over time.

Because engraving is best suited to simple artwork, logos or text with clear lines will produce the best results.
