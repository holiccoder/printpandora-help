![]({{IMAGE_PENDING:48452577374996}})What are Cotton Business Cards?
-------------------------------

[Cotton Business Cards](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/business-cards/cotton) are made from 100% tree-free cotton paper, created using cotton linters. These are the soft fibers that surround cotton seeds, giving the paper its natural texture and feel.

They’re uncoated, naturally bright white, and subtly textured.

Is this right for you?
----------------------

This works well if you want:

- A soft, tactile card that feels different from standard paper
- A natural, understated look
- A more sustainable, tree-free option

Compared to other options:

- Less glossy than Original or Super
- More textured and organic in feel
- Lighter than Luxe but still durable

  
At a glance
--------------

- **Paper weight:** 110lb / 298gsm / 17pt
- **Material:** 100% cotton (tree-free)
- **Finish:** Uncoated
- **Feel:** Soft with subtle texture
- **Recyclability:** 100% recyclable
- **Best for:** Minimal designs, handwritten notes

What makes the paper different?
----------------------------------

Cotton paper is made from **100% cotton linters**, making it completely tree-free.

- Naturally bright white with **no bleaching required**
- Soft, subtly textured surface
- Short fibers create a balance of **softness and strength**

To produce this paper:

- All pulp and raw materials are **sustainably sourced**
- Electricity used in manufacturing is matched with **Renewable Energy Credits (RECs)** from certified wind power

The updated process uses less energy and material than before, while maintaining the same look and feel.

Customization
-------------

- Uncoated surface gives a **muted, natural print result**
- Colors appear softer than on coated papers
- Works best with simple layouts and lighter ink coverage
