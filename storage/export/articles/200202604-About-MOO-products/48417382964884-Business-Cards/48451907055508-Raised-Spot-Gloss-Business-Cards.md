What is Raised Spot Gloss?
-----------------------------

Raised Spot Gloss (also called raised spot UV) is a clear coating applied to selected areas of your design.

It creates:

- A **raised, 3D-like texture** you can feel
- A **glossy shine** that catches the light
- A stronger contrast compared to standard Spot Gloss

  
Customization
----------------

- Applied only to selected parts of your design
- Can be used on **one or both sides** of the card
- Works best for logos, text, or bold shapes

Preparing your artwork
-------------------------

To set up a Raised Spot Gloss design, you’ll need two files:

- One for your **main printed design (background)**
- One for the **raised gloss layer** (showing where the finish will be applied)

![Design Guidelines](https://www.{{TEMPLATE_DOWNLOAD_URL}}/193f428f-5077-4fd9-81de-0c8eeb1fb461/US_spot_gloss.png)Your gloss artwork should be:

- **Vector-based**
- **Black and white only**
- Set to **100% black (CMYK 0/0/0/100)** for gloss areas
- Saved as a **PDF**

You can also use pre-made Raised Spot Gloss templates if you want a quicker starting point.

  
Design tips for best results
-------------------------------

Raised Spot Gloss works best when used as a highlight rather than a full layer.

- Avoid large solid areas of raised gloss
- Keep coverage to **less than 50% of each side**
- Use it to emphasize key elements like logos or headings
- Simpler shapes produce cleaner, more defined results

If you’re unsure, it’s worth reviewing the full **Special Finish Guidelines** before finalizing your design: [Special Finishes design requirements](https://{{HELP_DOMAIN}}/hc/articles/206347686)

  
What to know before ordering
-------------------------------

- Only one special finish can be applied per card
- Raised gloss adds both **visual and physical contrast**
- Best results come from clean, intentional designs
- Printed on Soft Touch Super paper for optimal effect
