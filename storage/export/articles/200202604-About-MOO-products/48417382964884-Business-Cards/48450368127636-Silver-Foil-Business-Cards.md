![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48451441537684)![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48451463654036)What are Silver Foil Business Cards?
------------------------------------

Silver Foil Business Cards are printed on thick Super paper (400gsm / 19pt) with a Soft Touch matte finish, then enhanced with metallic silver foil applied to selected parts of your design.

The foil is pressed onto the surface, creating a reflective, mirror-like effect that stands out against the matte background.

  
Is this right for you?
-------------------------

This works well if you want:

- A clean, modern metallic finish
- A high-contrast design with a cooler tone
- Key elements like logos or text to stand out

Compared to other finishes:

- Cooler and more understated than Gold Foil
- More reflective than Spot Gloss
- Less tactile than Raised Spot Gloss

  
At a glance
--------------

- **Paper:** Super (400gsm / 19pt)
- **Base finish:** Soft Touch matte
- **Finish type:** Metallic silver foil
- **Feel:** Smooth with reflective highlights
- **Material:** Made from responsibly sourced [FSC® certified material](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/38741101861652)
- **Best for:** Logos, accents, modern designs

Customization
----------------

- Silver foil is applied only to selected parts of your design
- Works best for bold shapes, text, or simple graphics
- Creates strong contrast against the matte background

![Printfinity.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48451333387796)While the front of every card in your pack will have the same image, you can have up to 50 different back designs when utilizing MOO’s own [Printfinity](https://www.moo.com/about/printfinity) option! *Please note, Printfinity options may be limited on some MOO Designs.*  
Preparing your artwork
-------------------------

To set up a design with silver foil, you’ll need to separate your artwork into layers:

- One file for your **main printed design (background)**
- One file for the **foil layer** (showing exactly where the foil will be applied)

![Design Guidelines](https://www.moo.com/dam/jcr:dd240ad8-a216-4a46-9a39-5a3f6c77eb43/US_silver.png)Your foil artwork should be:

- **Vector-based**
- **Black and white only**
- Set to **100% black (CMYK 0/0/0/100)** for the foil areas
- Saved as a **PDF**

If you’d rather not build this from scratch, you can use one of the available [Special Finish templates](https://www.moo.com/design-templates/tailored-business-cards/).


Design tips for best results
----------------------------

Special finishes work best when used intentionally.

- Use foil as an **accent**, not the entire design
- Avoid large solid areas of foil
- Keep the foil coverage to **less than 50% of each side**
- Simple, bold shapes will give the cleanest result

If you’re unsure, it’s worth reviewing the full **Special Finish Guidelines** before finalizing your design: [Special Finishes design requirements](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/206347686)

What to know before ordering
----------------------------

- You can only choose **one special finish per card**
- Fine details may not reproduce as cleanly in foil
- Templates and pre-made patterns are available if you need a starting point
- Special Finish cards are printed on Soft Touch Super paper for the best contrast and application

And yes… under the right light, the effect can be pretty striking.
