![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48452743105300)What are Letterpress Business Cards?
------------------------------------

[Letterpress Business Cards](https://www.moo.com/business-cards/letterpress) use a traditional printing technique where elements of the design are pressed into thick, soft paper. This creates a debossed impression you can both see and feel.

Unlike other business cards, **these are not fully customizable**. Instead, they come as a curated set of designs created specifically for the letterpress process.

  
Is this right for you?
-------------------------

This works well if you want:

- A refined, tactile finish with a pressed-in texture
- A premium card with a classic, understated look
- A professionally designed layout without needing to create your own

This may not be the right fit if you want:

- Full design control
- Custom artwork or branding applied
- Detailed or complex layouts

Compared to other options:

- More tactile than standard printed cards
- More structured and less flexible than Original, Super, or Luxe
- Focused on craftsmanship rather than customization

- **MOO (2.16" x 3.3")**
- **Square corners only**

  
What makes letterpress different?
------------------------------------

Letterpress is a physical printing process where the design is pressed into the paper.

This creates:

- A **debossed (pressed-in) texture**
- A **subtle depth and shadow effect**
- A finish that is tactile rather than glossy

Because of this process, designs are carefully controlled to ensure the best result.

  
Design and customization
---------------------------

Letterpress Business Cards are intentionally limited in customization.

- You can choose from [12 pre-designed templates](https://www.moo.com/design-templates/pdp_browse.php?productTypeName=businesscard&availability=letterpress)
- Each design is paired with a **complementary ink color or blind deboss**
- Layouts are optimized specifically for the letterpress process

It is **not possible to upload your own design or apply custom debossing or embossing** to these cards.

This approach ensures the final result is clean, balanced, and consistent with the capabilities of letterpress printing.

  
Paper and finish
-------------------

These cards are printed on **extra-thick 32pt Luxe paper**, made from Mohawk Superfine® stock.

- Uncoated and naturally textured
- Soft enough to hold a clean debossed impression
- Built with a **white seam** for a clean, minimal edge

The thickness and softness of the paper are key to achieving the letterpress effect.

  
What to know before ordering
-------------------------------

- Only available in MOO size with square corners
- You must choose from the available pre-designed layouts
- Custom artwork and branding cannot be applied
- Designs are intentionally minimal to suit the printing method
- This is a traditional print style focused on texture and craftsmanship rather than flexibility
