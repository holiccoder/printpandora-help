What are Gold Foil Business Cards?
-------------------------------------

[Gold Foil Business Cards](https://www.moo.com/us/business-cards/finishes/gold-foil-business-cards) are printed on thick Super paper (400gsm / 19pt) with a Soft Touch matte finish, then enhanced with metallic gold foil applied to selected parts of your design.

The foil is pressed into the surface, creating a warm, reflective finish that catches the light and stands out against the matte background.

  
Is this right for you?
-------------------------

This works well if you want:

- A bold, attention-grabbing finish
- A warmer, more decorative metallic look
- Key elements like logos or names to stand out

Compared to other finishes:

- Warmer and more expressive than Silver Foil
- More reflective than Spot Gloss
- Less tactile than Raised Spot Gloss

  
At a glance
--------------

- **Paper:** Super (400gsm / 19pt)
- **Base finish:** Soft Touch matte
- **Finish type:** Metallic gold foil
- **Feel:** Smooth with reflective highlights
- **Material:** Made from responsibly sourced [FSC® certified material](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/38741101861652)
- **Best for:** Logos, accents, statement designs

Customization
----------------

- Gold foil is applied only to selected parts of your design
- Works best for bold shapes, text, or simple graphics
- Creates strong contrast against the matte background

Preparing your artwork
-------------------------

To set up a design with gold foil, you’ll need to separate your artwork into layers:

- One file for your **main printed design (background)**
- One file for the **foil layer** (showing exactly where the foil will be applied)

![Design Guidelines](https://www.moo.com/dam/jcr:42c4e407-068d-4dbf-85d4-e8938829da67/US_gold.png)Your foil artwork should be:

- **Vector-based**
- **Black and white only**
- Set to **100% black (CMYK 0/0/0/100)** for the foil areas
- Saved as a **PDF**

If you’d rather not build this from scratch, you can use one of the available [Special Finish templates](https://www.moo.com/design-templates/tailored-business-cards/).

Design tips for best results
----------------------------

Gold foil works best when used as an accent rather than the main feature.

- Use foil as an **accent**, not the entire design
- Avoid large solid areas of foil
- Keep the foil coverage to **less than 50% of each side**
- Simple, bold shapes will give the cleanest result

If you’re unsure, it’s worth reviewing the full **Special Finish Guidelines** before finalizing your design: [Special Finishes design requirements](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/206347686)

What to know before ordering
----------------------------

- You can only choose **one special finish per card**
- Fine details may not reproduce as cleanly in foil
- Templates and patterns are available if you need a starting point
- Special Finish cards are printed on Soft Touch Super paper for the best contrast and application

Under the right light, the gold foil will catch and reflect, giving your design a noticeable edge without needing extra color.
