![MERCH-650-Paper-rich-media-4-Original-1152x576.jpg]({{IMAGE_PENDING:48442815481492}})What are Original Business Cards?
---------------------------------

[Original Business Cards](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/business-cards/original) are the classic {{BRAND_NAME}} option. They’re made from 350gsm (16pt) paper and are designed to balance print quality, durability, and value.

They use a coated paper stock, which gives a smooth finish and helps colors appear crisp and vibrant.

  
Is this right for you?
-------------------------

This works well if you want:

- A reliable, everyday business card
- Strong color reproduction for detailed designs
- Flexibility between matte and gloss finishes

Compared to other options:

- More vibrant than Cotton due to the coated surface
- Lighter and more affordable than Super and Luxe
- Less textured than Cotton or Luxe

What to know before ordering
-------------------------------

- Matte is easier to write on than gloss
- Gloss can show fingerprints more easily
- Best all-around option if you’re unsure which paper to choose
