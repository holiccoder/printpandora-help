![MERCH-650-Paper-rich-media-4-Original-1152x576.jpg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48442815481492)What are Original Business Cards?
---------------------------------

[Original Business Cards](https://www.moo.com/business-cards/original) are the classic MOO option. They’re made from 350gsm (16pt) paper and are designed to balance print quality, durability, and value.

They use a coated paper stock, which gives a smooth finish and helps colors appear crisp and vibrant.

  
Is this right for you?
-------------------------

This works well if you want:

- A reliable, everyday business card
- Strong color reproduction for detailed designs
- Flexibility between matte and gloss finishes

Compared to other options:

- More vibrant than Cotton due to the coated surface
- Lighter and more affordable than Super and Luxe
- Less textured than Cotton or Luxe

  
At a glance
--------------

- **Paper weight:** 350gsm / 16pt
- **Finish options:**

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48419147471124)**Matte**

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48419163914388)**Gloss**

- **Feel:** Smooth, coated
- **Material:**
    - Made from responsibly sourced [FSC® certified material ](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/38741101861652)
    - Sustainable source, Elemental Chlorine Free (ECF)
- **Best for:** Everyday use, detailed designs, bold colors

Customization
----------------

- **Matte:** Softer, non-reflective finish
- **Gloss:** Shiny finish that enhances color contrast

![Printfinity.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/48419972659732)While the front of every card in your pack will have the same image, you can have up to 50 different back designs when utilizing MOO’s own [Printfinity](https://www.moo.com/about/printfinity) option! *Please note, Printfinity options may be limited on some MOO Designs.*  
What to know before ordering
-------------------------------

- Matte is easier to write on than gloss
- Gloss can show fingerprints more easily
- Best all-around option if you’re unsure which paper to choose
