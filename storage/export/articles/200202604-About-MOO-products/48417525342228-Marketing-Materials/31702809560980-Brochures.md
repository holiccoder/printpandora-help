### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the minimum order quantity for printed Brochures?**The minimum order is **25 brochures**.**Can I order more than 10,000 Brochures?****Yes!** For larger orders or custom printing options, please **contact us at <inquiries@moo.com>**.**How can I customize the Brochures?**Upload your design, and we’ll print it in vibrant, full color on both sides.**What are the dimensions of the Brochures?**- **Unfolded**: US Letter size (8.5" x 11")
- **Bi-fold**: 5.5" x 8.5"
- **Tri-fold**: 3.67" x 8.5"

**Which paper stocks are available?**- **Premium Matte Paper:** 80lb (225gsm) 
    - Smooth, natural finish
- **Premium Gloss Paper:** 100lb (250gsm) 
    - Glossy, vibrant colors

**What colors are the Brochures available in?**We start with a **blank white canvas**. Add your custom design to make them yours!**Do I need to fold my custom Brochures myself?****No!** We score and fold the Brochures so they’re ready to use upon arrival.**Can I get a sample?**We can send you a **MOO-branded sample** within a week. Just email <inquiries@moo.com>.**Can I write on the Brochures?**Our coated paper has a **smooth finish, which can make writing tricky.** If you need a pen-friendly option, we suggest our **Premium Matte Flyers**.**Do you offer other sizes, like A4?****Yes!** We offer **A4 Brochures** through our **exclusive Offline Catalog**, shipped from our UK facility. Email <inquiries@moo.com> to learn more.**Do you offer other fold types?**Currently, we offer **Bi-fold** and **Tri-fold** options only.**Can the Brochures be mailed directly to multiple addresses?**Unfortunately, we **do not offer mailing services at this time**.**Why are the artwork guidelines two pages for Brochures?**The guidelines cover both the inside and outside of the brochure. This is especially important for Tri-folds, where panel sizes vary slightly.**How long does it take to receive my Brochures?**Printing takes **5 business days**. Once shipped, you will receive tracking information via email.
