What are Vinyl Stickers?
------------------------

[Vinyl Stickers](https://www.moo.com/us/stickers/vinyl-stickers) are a durable, versatile choice for everyday use. They come in a range of standard shapes and sizes, with a smooth matte finish you can write on when you need to add a personal note, label or detail.

Perfect for packaging, laptops, envelopes, product labels and more, they’re a practical option when you want something that feels polished but works hard. More durable than paper stickers and more visible than clear stickers, they’re a reliable middle ground for all kinds of projects.

Simple to use. Easy to love.

  
Is this the right choice for you?
------------------------------------

Vinyl Stickers are ideal if you’re:

- Using stickers across different surfaces, from packaging to laptops
- Looking for something more durable than paper
- Ordering multiple designs in one batch
- Wanting a matte surface you can write on

Compared to paper stickers, Vinyl Stickers stand up better to handling and moisture. Compared to clear stickers, they’re fully opaque, so your design stays crisp, bold and easy to spot.

No disappearing act. Just your design, front and centre.

  
When to consider something else
----------------------------------

You might want a different option if:

- You need a custom shape that follows your artwork (die cut stickers handle that better)
- You want a “no-label” transparent look (clear stickers are designed for that)
- You’re prioritizing recyclability or a paper-based feel (matte or coated paper stickers are better suited)

  
At a glance
--------------

- **Material**: Vinyl
- **Finish**: Matte (writeable surface)
- **Durability**: Water-resistant and suitable for everyday handling
- **Format**: Available in multiple standard shapes and sizes
- **Design flexibility**: Supports multiple unique designs per order (Printfinity)

How they compare
-------------------

- Compared to [clear stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/46009040781588), these are fully opaque and designed to stand out rather than blend in
- Compared to [coated paper stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/38136760481300), these are typically more flexible and suited for regular handling
- Compared to [metallic stickers](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/39104152675092), these are more practical than decorative

If you’re unsure where to start, vinyl is usually the safest “middle ground” across durability, flexibility, and ease of use.

  
Customization: what to expect
--------------------------------

Vinyl stickers are straightforward to design and use:

- Your artwork prints directly onto the matte vinyl surface
- The finish makes it easy to write on with pen or marker
- You can include multiple designs in one order, depending on the format

This makes them especially useful for brands that want variation without placing separate orders.

  
Common questions
-------------------

**Can I include multiple designs in one order?**  
Yes. The number of designs depends on the format. For example, smaller formats support more variations, while larger formats support fewer.

- **Round Stickers:** up to 52 unique designs.
- **Large Round Stickers:** up to 12 unique designs.
- **Rectangular Stickers:** up to 50 unique designs.
- **Large Rectangular Stickers:** up to 25 unique designs.
- **StickerBooks:** up to 90 unique designs.
- **Return Address Label:** up to 125 unique designs.

*Please note, Printfinity options may be limited on MOO Designs.*

**Are these waterproof?**  
They’re water-resistant and hold up well for everyday use. If you need long-term outdoor durability, clear or other specialty vinyl options may be a better fit.

**Can I write on them?**  
Yes. The matte finish is designed to be writeable.
