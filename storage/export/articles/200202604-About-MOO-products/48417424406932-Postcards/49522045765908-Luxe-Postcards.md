What makes Luxe different?
--------------------------

Luxe cards are built using a layered construction, which is what gives them their distinctive thickness and edge colour.

Here’s how the layers in the image come together:  
![0296WF-RM-608x456-luxe-diagram.png]({{IMAGE_PENDING:49522035480852}})

- **(1) Top outer layer**  
    Made from uncoated {{PAPER_BRAND}} Superfine® paper. This is the surface you print on, with a natural texture and a soft, tactile feel.
- **(2) coloured seam (middle layers)**  
    The inner layers create the signature coloured edge. This is a solid insert, not printed ink, which is why the colour appears so bold and consistent around the sides of the card.
- **(3) Bottom outer layer**  
    Matches the top layer, giving the card a consistent uncoated finish on both sides. It’s also writable, making it useful for notes or added details.

This layered structure is what makes Luxe noticeably thicker, more rigid, and more tactile than any other business card option.
