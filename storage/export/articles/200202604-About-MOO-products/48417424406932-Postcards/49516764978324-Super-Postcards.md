Gold and Silver Foil options
-------------------------------

Super Postcards can be finished with shiny Gold Foil or Silver Foil. These special finishes work best as accents, adding a bright metallic detail to selected parts of your design.

To get the best result, avoid large, solid blocks of foil. Try to balance standard print with special finish. In total, each side of your design must contain less than 50% special finish.
