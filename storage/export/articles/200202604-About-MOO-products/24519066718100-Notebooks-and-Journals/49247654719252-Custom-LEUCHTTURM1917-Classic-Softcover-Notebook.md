A notebook you'll actually want to use every day. **The LEUCHTTURM1917 Classic Softcover Notebook** brings together thoughtful design, quality materials and your branding in one compact package.

**What you get**
----------------





This is a medium-sized notebook built for people who take notes seriously. At 5.75" x 8.25", it's big enough to be useful and easy enough to carry around. The flexible faux vegan leather cover feels great in hand, and the lay-flat binding means it stays open on your desk without a fight. Inside, you get 123 lined pages in a slightly chamois-colored paper that reduces glare and is easy on the eyes. The notebook also comes with two ribbon bookmarks, 8 perforated tear-out sheets, and a gusseted back pocket for cards or loose notes.

**Customization**
-----------------

Make it yours. The LEUCHTTURM1917 Classic Softcover can be customized in two ways:

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/49247703539988)**Single Color Printing** adds your logo or artwork directly to the cover in one color.

![](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/49247703542292)**Blind Debossing** presses your design into the cover without ink, creating a subtle, tactile impression.

**What you can customize:** Logo, artwork, or text on the cover. Colors available in Black and Navy.

**The process:** Upload your artwork through MOO's online design tool. Templates are available to help you set things up properly.

**Artwork guidance:** For best results, use high-resolution artwork at 300dpi. Vector files are preferred for logos and text, so everything comes out sharp.
