**What colors are the MOO Softcover Notebooks available in?**Choose from three sophisticated colors: **Jet Black**, **Cloudy Grey**, and **Midnight Blue**.**Can I get a sample?**Absolutely. We can ship you a MOO-branded sample within a week. Just email [**inquiries@moo.com**](mailto:inquiries@moo.com).**What materials are the MOO Softcover Notebooks made of?**The cover is crafted from **Ecorel**—a recyclable, paper-based faux leather made from **50% post-consumer waste**. The pages are premium **Munken Kristall** text paper.**Is there any volume discount if I order more?**Yes, we offer volume discounts. Email [**inquiries@moo.com**](mailto:inquiries@moo.com) to learn more.**Can I mix the MOO Softcover Notebook colors and finishes within my order?**Currently, we can only process **one color and finish per order**.**How many pages are in the notebook?**Each notebook contains **144 pages**.**What is the page weight?**Pages are printed on smooth **Munken Kristall 100gsm** paper.**What is the line spacing for the MOO Softcover Notebook?**Line spacing is a comfortable **6mm**.**How are the MOO Softcover Notebooks packaged?**Each notebook comes in an individual **compostable bag**, and your entire order is securely boxed.**What sizes of the MOO Softcover Notebook are available?**- **Medium** (A5)
- **Large** (A4)

**What are the weights of each MOO Softcover Notebook?**- Medium (A5): **9.91 oz (281g)**
- Large (A4): **19.68 oz (558g)**

**How is the foil applied to the Softcover Notebook?**We use **hot foil stamping** to apply the foil design to the cover.**How does the hot foil stamping appear on the Softcover Notebook?**Due to the tactile texture of the cover, the foil may reveal subtle textures when viewed up close, adding to its unique charm.**What size text and lines should I use in my designs?**For optimal results, use font sizes above **10pt** and line thicknesses above **1pt**. For debossing, keep solid designs under **1" x 1"**, and for foil, under **0.5" x 0.5"**.
