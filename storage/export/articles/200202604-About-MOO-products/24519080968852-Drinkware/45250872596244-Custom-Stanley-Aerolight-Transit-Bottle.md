With its leakproof lid and Stanley’s ‘featherweight’ technology, the Aerolight™ Transit is a packable, portable bottle that’s perfect for daily commutes.

**What is this product?**
-------------------------

A lightweight, insulated bottle designed to move with you. The [Stanley Aerolight™ Transit Bottle](https://www.moo.com/us/new/products/stanley-aerolight-transit-bottle/black-one-sided-engraving-20oz) keeps drinks hot or cold for hours, without the bulk of a traditional stainless steel bottle.

It’s built for everyday carry. Slim enough for cup holders. Tough enough for daily use. And noticeably lighter than standard insulated bottles.

**Is it right for you?**
---------------------------

Perfect if you’re looking for something easy to carry, easy to store, and reliable throughout the day.

Works well if you:

- Commute regularly and want a bottle that won’t weigh you down
- Need something that fits neatly in your bag or car cup holder
- Switch between hot coffee and cold water throughout the day

Less ideal if you’re after larger capacity drinkware. This one is designed for portability first.

**Product details**
-------------------

**Lightweight design**  
One third lighter than standard Stanley stainless steel bottles. Easier to carry, without compromising durability.

**Leakproof lid**  
Built to travel. Toss it in your bag without worrying about spills.

**Temperature control**  
Keeps drinks cold for 12 hours, or up to 48 hours with ice. Hot drinks stay warm for up to 8 hours.

**Everyday compatibility**  
Slim profile fits standard cup holders, so it works just as well in the car as it does on your desk.


**Customization**
-----------------

Make it yours, or make it branded.

You can choose between:

- **One-sided engraving** – A subtle, premium finish etched into the surface. Clean, durable, and built to last.
- **One-sided printing** – A bold, visible design applied to the surface. Great for logos or standout artwork.

Both options are applied to one side of the bottle.
