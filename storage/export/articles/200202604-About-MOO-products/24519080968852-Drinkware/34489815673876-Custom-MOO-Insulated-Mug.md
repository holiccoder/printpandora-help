**What is the minimum order quantity for custom Custom Insulated Mugs?**The minimum order quantity is **10 mugs**.**How can I customize the Insulated Mug?**Customize your mug with **one-sided engraving** for a personal touch.**What are the dimensions of the Insulated Mug?**The dimensions are **85mm x 115mm x 85mm**.**What colors are available?**Choose between **Jet Black** and **Cloudy Grey**.**What’s the capacity of the Insulated Mug?**It holds **12oz / 340ml** of your favorite beverage.**How much does the Insulated Mug weigh?**Each mug weighs **273g / 9.63oz**.**How long will my beverage stay cold?**Keep your drink chilled for up to **24 hours**—a whole Earth day.**How long will my beverage stay hot?**Enjoy warmth for up to **12 hours**—about the length of a day on Saturn.**Are the Insulated Mugs BPA free?**Yes, all **MOO Drinkware** is **BPA-free**.**What is the Insulated Mug made of?**- **Body:** 18/8 powder-coated stainless steel
- **Lid:** Clear plastic with silicone gaskets for a secure fit

- **Microwave:** Not suitable

**Can I get a sample?**- Yes! Request a **MOO-branded sample** by emailing [**inquiries@moo.com**](mailto:inquiries@moo.com)—it ships within a week.
