**What is this product?**
-------------------------

The [Stanley Stay-Chill Stacking Pint](https://www.moo.com/us/new/products/stanley-stay-chill-stacking-pint/green-one-sided-engraving-16oz) is a 16oz insulated drinkware option designed to keep drinks cold for hours. No lid. No fuss. Just a solid, reliable pint that does its job well.
