What Are Display Boxes Made Of?
-------------------------------

All our Display Boxes are made with premium Japanese paper that's 50% recycled and are manufactured in China.

 
-

Are They Plastic-Free?
----------------------

Yes, the Display Boxes contain no plastic whatsoever—they even arrive packed in a compostable bag.

 
-

Do the Display Boxes Include Any {{BRAND_NAME}} Branding?
----------------------------------------------

Yes, the Display Boxes are branded with a small {{BRAND_NAME}} logo on the back. This cannot be removed.

Can I Customize Display Boxes?
