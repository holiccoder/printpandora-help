 The MOO Water Bottle is double-walled to keep your water cool for up to 24 hours. Perfect for long days at a hot desk.**Are these water bottles BPA free?** Yes, all MOO Water bottles are BPA (Bisphenol A) free.   **How much does the MOO Water Bottle weigh?** 10.9oz on its own (or 15.7oz with packaging)   **What’s the opening width of the MOO Water Bottle?** The outer diameter of the drinking mouth is 1.38”.   **Can I customize the bottle?** You can indeed. There is a minimum quantity for customization. You can find all the details [here](https://www.moo.com/us/drinkware/custom-moo-water-bottles).  **Can I put my MOO Water Bottle in the refrigerator, freezer or microwave?** You can put your MOO Water Bottle in the refrigerator or freezer, but not in the microwave (as it could damage the bottle’s vacuum insulation).   **Can I put my MOO Water Bottle in the dishwasher?** MOO Water Bottles are not dishwasher safe. Hand wash only, please.   **What liquids can I fill my MOO Water Bottle with?** Apart from milk, carbonated drinks and some juices, you can fill your MOO Water Bottle with all your favourite drinks.   **How long will my beverage stay cold?** Up to 24 hours. A whole Earth day.   **How long will my beverage stay hot?** Up to 12 hours. (About the length of a day on Saturn.)   **What material is the MOO Water Bottle made of?** It’s made from 18/8 stainless steel, with a stainless steel lining for a clean taste. It has passed both FDA and LFGB tests (and it’s BPA and toxin free).    **What’s the capacity of the MOO Water Bottle?** 17oz (or 500ml)   **Where is the MOO Water Bottle manufactured?** Designed in the United Kingdom, every MOO Water Bottle is responsibly made in China.
