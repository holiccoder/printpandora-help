With normal use, your MOO Water Bottle should last you many years. To keep it in the best condition, please follow these care instructions:

Do's
----

- **Hand wash your MOO Water Bottle.** This helps maintain its quality and longevity.
- **Ensure the lid is properly closed and airtight.** This prevents spills and leaks.

 
-

Don'ts
------

- **Don't put your MOO Water Bottle in the microwave.** It's not microwave-safe.
- **Don't overfill it with hot liquids.** Leave some space to prevent spills and potential burns.
- **Don't store liquids in your bottle long-term.** Empty and clean it regularly to prevent odors and bacteria growth.
- **Don't fill it with carbonated drinks.** The pressure can build up and cause the lid to fail.
- **Don't use juices or milk-based drinks.** These can spoil and create pressure inside the bottle.

 
-

Important Safety Information
----------------------------

Please use your MOO Water Bottle only for its intended purpose—holding standard beverages like water, tea, or coffee. Misuse could damage the bottle and potentially cause injury.
