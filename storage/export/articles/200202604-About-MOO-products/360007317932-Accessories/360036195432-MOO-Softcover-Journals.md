Our Softcover Journals measure 5.12" x 8.15" and contain 64 pages:

**Lined**

- 60 lined or dotted pages (100gsm) with a smooth, non-glare surface  
    
    - **Lines**: 29 lines
    - **Spacing**: 6mm
- 4 Grey blank pages (135gsm) in the middle of the Journal

**Dotted**

- 60 lined or dotted pages (100gsm) with a smooth, non-glare surface 
    - **Dots**: 24 dots across by 37 dots down
    - **Spacing**: 5mm
- 4 Grey blank pages (135gsm) in the middle of the Journal
