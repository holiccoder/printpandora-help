Our Hardcover Notebooks measure 5.12" x 8.15" and contain 176 pages:

**Lined**

- 160 lined pages (100gsm) with a smooth, non-glare writing surface 
    - **Lines**: 29 lines
    - **Spacing**: 6mm
- 16 unlined pages (135gsm) in the middle of the Notebook creating a central seam in a contrasting color to the Notebook cover.

**Dotted**

- 160 dotted pages (100gsm) with a smooth, non-glare writing surface 
    - **Dots**: 23 dots across by 35 dots down
    - **Spacing**: 5mm
- 16 unlined pages (135gsm) in the middle of the Notebook creating a central seam in a contrasting color to the Notebook cover.
