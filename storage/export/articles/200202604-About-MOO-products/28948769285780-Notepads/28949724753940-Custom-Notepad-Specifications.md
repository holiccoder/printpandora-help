   Keep your brand front and center with these premium, tearable Notepads. Available in two sizes, Small and Medium, with 50 or 100 pages.

**What is the minimum order quantity for custom Notepads?**

 There is a minimum order quantity of **50 Notepads**.

 **How can I customize the Notepads?**

 You can customize your Notepads with a **single design** of your choosing. It will be printed on every sheet of the Notepad.

 **What are the dimensions of the Notepads?** - Small: **4" x 6"**
- Medium: **5" x 7"**

 **What colors are the Notepads available in?**

 Notepads start off as **blank white paper**, but you can upload your own design to bring them to life!

   **Can I get a sample?**

 To have a **MOO-branded sample** shipped to you, please email <inquiries@moo.com>

   **How many pages are in the Notepad?**

 You can choose either **50** or **100 pages**.

   **Can I remove the reorder prompt sheet?**

 No, the **reorder prompt sheet is fixed**. This doesn’t count towards your page limit.

   **What is the lead time for printed Notepads?** Notepads take **5 business days to print** before they're shipped to you.

  **Can I print on both sides?** Unfortunately, no. Only **single-sided customization** is available for this product.

  **Do you offer Printfinity on the Notepad?** No, **Printfinity is not an option** with this product. A single design is printed on every sheet of your Notepad.

  **Can I order a different size?** Notepads are only available in **4" x 6” or 5" x 7” sizes**.

   **Can I order a different number of pages in the Notepad?** No. At the moment our Notepads are only offered with **50** or **100 pages**.   **How do the Notepads come packaged?** They’re boxed with a **cardboard sheet** to protect them in transit.   **What are the weights of the Notepads?** Small with 50 sheets: **2.7oz**  
Small with 100 sheets: **4.9oz**  
Medium with 50 sheets: **3.8oz**  
Medium with 100 sheets: **7.2oz**   **What materials are the Notepads made of?** **70 lb** (104gsm) **Accent Opaque Warm White** text paper with an **80lb White C2S backing**.   **Do you deliver Notepads outside of the US?** Currently, our platform can only take orders shipping **within the United States**. If you need to ship abroad, please get in contact with our Sales Team at <inquiries@moo.com>
