How do Special Finishes work?
-----------------------------

Special Finishes are applied as a separate layer during production. That means:

- They aren’t printed in standard ink.
- You’ll need to upload artwork that clearly shows where the finish should go.
- Very fine lines or tiny text may not work well.

Each product page includes specific artwork guidelines to help you set up your file correctly.

Can I use Special Finishes anywhere in my design?
-------------------------------------------------

There are a few limitations to make sure everything prints cleanly:

- Minimum line thickness applies
- Small or intricate details may not hold the finish
- Finished areas shouldn’t overlap in unsupported ways

We recommend using Special Finishes on bold, simple elements for the best results.

Do Special Finish products cost more?
-------------------------------------

Yes. Because they require extra materials and production steps, Special Finish products are priced higher than standard print.
