Create your artwork in **CMYK**, using vector files where possible or raster images at **300 dpi** or higher. Make sure lines are **at least 0.65 pt thick**, avoid transparency effects, and **outline all fonts** at a minimum of 6 pt. If your design wraps onto the back, ensure both sides align seamlessly, then remove the info layer and export your file as a PDF/X-1a.\* may appear blank until opened in Adobe software. Please do not save your design in the 'artwork guidelines' group layer or it will not print.
