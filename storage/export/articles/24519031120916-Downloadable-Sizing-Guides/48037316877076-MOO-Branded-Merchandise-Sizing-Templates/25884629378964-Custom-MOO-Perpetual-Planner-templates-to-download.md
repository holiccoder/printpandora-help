**Full Print (Soft Cover) Perpetual Planner Sizing Templates**
-----------------------------------------------------------------

![Illustrator-01.png](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/25884605279380) [Adobe Illustrator Templates (pdf/ai)](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29648834098580)\*![perpetual-planners-full-cover-526x325.jpg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29648849668116)![Photoshop-01.png](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/25884629373332) [Adobe Photoshop Templates (psd)](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29648834103828)![InDesign-01.png](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/25884605282580) [Adobe InDesign Templates (indd)](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29648849677844)\*![JPG-01.png](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29648834092180) [JPEG Templates (jpeg)](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29648834122516)Set up your full wrap artwork to **314 mm × 243 mm (bleed)**, keeping key content within the **264 mm × 193 mm safe area** across the front, back, and spine to avoid trimming or folding issues. For the topsheet, use **132 mm × 210 mm (bleed)** with a **112 mm × 190 mm safe area**, making sure all important elements stay inside this space.  
Create your files in **CMYK**, outline all fonts, and export as a **PDF/X-1a** (or high-resolution JPEG if there’s no text). Extend backgrounds fully to the bleed, avoid adding extra crop marks, and remove guideline layers before saving to ensure clean, accurate printing.\* may appear blank until opened in Adobe software. Please do not save your design in the 'artwork guidelines' group layer or it will not print.
