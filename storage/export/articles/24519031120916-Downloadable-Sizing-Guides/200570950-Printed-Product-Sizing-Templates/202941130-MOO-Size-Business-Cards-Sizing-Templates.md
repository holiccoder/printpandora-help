[JPEG Templates (jpeg)](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/33703505802004)If you are uploading photography or non-vector artwork (e.g. high-quality JPEGs), please make sure they are no less than 333 x 223 pixels at 96dpi or ideally sized at **1038 x 696 pixels at 300dpi**. If your designs are a mix of photography and design, make sure the embedded photographs used are above the recommended pixel sizes.

\* may appear blank until opened in Adobe software. Please do not save your design in the 'artwork guidelines' group layer or it will not print.
