Have some cool line art you want to print in Gold Foil, Silver Foil, or Spot Gloss? Here are a few things to keep in mind when preparing your artwork.

![icon__ui--warning-circle-bold-red.svg]({{IMAGE_PENDING:45371249476500}})**Using our Special Finishes as an Enhancement**  
Special finishes work best as design accents. Avoid large, solid blocks of gold foil or spot gloss; try to find a balance of standard print and special finish. In total, each side of your design must contain less than 50% special finish.![01_SF_icon.svg]({{IMAGE_PENDING:29834232781076}}) ![02_SF_icon.svg]({{IMAGE_PENDING:29834232780436}})**Line weight 1pt or higher**

Thicker lines print better. We recommend 1pt and above

 **Font size 10pt or higher**

10pt and higher. Otherwise, the small gaps in typography will cause pooling

![03_SF_icon.svg]({{IMAGE_PENDING:29834273510036}}) ![04_SF_icon.svg]({{IMAGE_PENDING:29834273511572}})**Vector graphics**

Create your special finish in vector to ensure clean, crisp lines

 **Small gaps**

Avoid small gaps less than 2pt wide to prevent pooling

**Line Art**

        ![03_SF_icon.svg]({{IMAGE_PENDING:29834273512212}})     Use **vector-based PDF** artwork for the element layer.

 Low-quality artwork can make the Foils or Gloss come out jagged and vector PDFs will help prevent this.
