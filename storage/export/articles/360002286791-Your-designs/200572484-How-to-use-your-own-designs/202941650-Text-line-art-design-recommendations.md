There are some key points to remember when using text/line art in your designs:

![120.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29834295117204) ![110.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29834304656276)Make sure text is at least **8pt** - very small text can look good on screen but does not print well.

 Make sure all fonts are **embedded**/**outlined/ converted to shape**.

![060.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29834295111828) ![090.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29834304653972)When printing lines in a solid or dark color, make sure they are no thinner than **0.5pt**. If you're using a lighter color, we recommend **1pt**.

 Make artwork a **vector PDF**, photographs at **300dpi** or higher, and file size a maximum of **25Mb**.

![100.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29834304654740) ![020.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29834304650644)Try to keep text in bold colors, made up of one or two inks only (**C, M, Y, or K**). Fonts using 100% K will be crisper and more legible

 **Remove any cutting, crop, or trim marks**. These are applied by MOO, as we send your files to print.

![050.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29834304651028) **![080.svg](https://support.moo.com/hc/article_attachments/29834295113108)**Save your designs using the '[Adobe PDF/X-1a:2001](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/202838774)' preset, where possible.

 To make sure your design prints as expected **flatten** **transparencies** and **turn off overprint**

If you want to try your existing design on one of our templates, head over to the [artwork guidelines and templates FAQ](https://support.moo.com/hc/categories/360002286791).
