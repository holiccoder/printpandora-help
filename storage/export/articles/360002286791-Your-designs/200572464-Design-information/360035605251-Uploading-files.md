When creating a new product online, you can upload images, logos, or complete design files in several ways:

Upload Options
--------------

- **From Your Computer:** Upload files directly from your device.
- **Flickr:** Connect to your Flickr account to import images.
- **Etsy:** Use images from your Etsy store.

You can also create a unique **QR code** that links to a website, phone number, or vCard details.

 
-

Accepted File Types
-------------------

We accept the following file formats:

- **PDF**
- **JPEG**
- **PNG**

 
-

File Storage and GDPR Compliance
--------------------------------

Any files you upload will be stored in the file library during your browser session. This allows you to test different files without re-uploading each time.

**Important Note:** In line with GDPR regulations, these library files will be removed at the end of each browser session. If you close your browser or restart your device, you will need to re-upload any files you wish to use.

However, any files uploaded to a **saved project** will remain in your account for future use.
