To ensure the best print quality for your products, please follow these guidelines when uploading your designs.

  
Image Resolution Requirements
--------------------------------

If you're uploading photography or non-vector artwork (e.g., JPEGs, PNGs), make sure your images are at least **96 dpi**. However, for optimal print quality, we highly recommend using images at **300 dpi**. Higher resolution images result in sharper and more professional-looking prints.

If your designs are a mix of photography and design elements, ensure that all embedded photographs meet or exceed the recommended pixel dimensions listed below.


Maximum File Size
-----------------

Please note that individual files must be less than **50 MB**. Files larger than this cannot be uploaded to {{BRAND_NAME}}.

Why High Resolution Matters
---------------------------

Using high-resolution images ensures that your printed products look sharp and professional. Low-resolution images may appear pixelated or blurry when printed.


How to Check Image Resolution in Common Design Software
-------------------------------------------------------

To make sure your images meet the recommended resolution, you can check the dpi (dots per inch) in your design software. Here's how:

### Adobe Photoshop

1. Open your image in Photoshop.
2. Go to **Image &gt; Image Size**.
3. In the dialog box, look for the **Resolution** field.
4. Ensure the resolution is set to **300 pixels/inch**.

### Adobe Illustrator

1. Open your document in Illustrator.
2. Go to **Effect &gt; Document Raster Effects Settings**.
3. In the dialog box, set the **Resolution** to **300 ppi**.
4. Click **OK**.

### Adobe InDesign

1. Open your document in InDesign.
2. Go to **Window &gt; Output &gt; Preflight**.
3. In the Preflight panel, click on the **Links and Images** section.
4. Check the **Effective PPI** of your images; they should be at least **300 ppi**.

### Other Software

If you're using other design software, look for image or document properties that show the resolution or dpi/ppi settings. Ensure that your images are set to 300 dpi for optimal quality.

We want your final print to look great; therefore, we do not recommend using AI-generated images. AI-generated files are not necessarily designed for printing and are typically suitable for digital viewing. Many AI-generated images are low-resolution, and when printed, the result may be blurry or pixelated. Additionally, designs generated with AI tools aren’t always compatible with our specific design requirements.

### Steps to Convert Image Resolution in Photoshop

1. Open your image in Photoshop.
2. Go to **Image &gt; Image Size**.
3. Uncheck the **Resample** option to maintain image quality.
4. Change the **Resolution** to **300 pixels/inch**.
5. Click **OK**.

If the image dimensions become too small after changing the resolution, you may need to find a higher-resolution version of the image.

### Tips for Improving Image Resolution

- **Use Original Images:** Work with the original, highest-resolution files whenever possible.
- **Avoid Scaling Up:** Enlarging low-resolution images can lead to pixelation and loss of quality.
- **Scan at High Resolution:** If scanning images, set the scanner to at least 300 dpi.
