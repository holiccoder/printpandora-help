We’ve created a range of [Spot Gloss](https://www.moo.com/us/create/hub/tailored-business-cards/?product_type=businesscard_us&element=spot_uv_2d&corner_type=square_corners), [Raised Spot Gloss, ](https://www.moo.com/us/create/hub/tailored-business-cards/?product_type=businesscard_us&element=spot_uv_3d&corner_type=square_corners)and [Gold Foil](https://www.moo.com/us/create/hub/tailored-business-cards/?product_type=businesscard_us&element=foil_gold&corner_type=square_corners) designs for you to choose from. You can toggle between card size, Special Finishes, and corner type at the top of the page.

There currently aren't any MOO Designs available for our Silver Foil Business Cards or Special Finish Postcards but we're hoping to add some soon.

If you’d prefer to get creative, you can find detailed instructions on how to prep your artwork [here](https://support.moo.com/hc/articles/206347686-Special-Finishes-design-requirements).

If you’re looking for some patterns to add over your designs, we’ve got some [patterns you can download](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/202135913/MOO_Tailored_Patterns.zip). You can then upload them to the Gold Foil or Spot Gloss layer of your design.
