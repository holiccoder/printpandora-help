When submitting an order online and entering your billing/shipping details you will be given an option to save and remove any addresses.

Should you wish to remove or edit any of these saved addresses after ordering, you can do so from the **Address Book** at the bottom right-hand side of your [Account Overview](https://www.moo.com/account/) when logged in.
