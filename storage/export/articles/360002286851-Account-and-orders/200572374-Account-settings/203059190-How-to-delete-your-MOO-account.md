Want to delete your MOO account? Please get in touch with our [Customer Service](https://www.moo.com/contact) team if you have any worries or concerns with your account. If something at MOO.com has troubled you, we'll be happy to help sort it out or delete your account for you.

In the meantime, why not check our [premium printed products](https://www.moo.com/products)?

**GDPR right to erasure**

**If you wish to [exercise your right to erasure](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001072603) (also known as the ‘right to be forgotten’) and have all your MOO data removed in line with GDPR, please email our data privacy manager directly at <data.privacymanager@moo.com>.**
