If you would like to change your account credentials, sign into your account. Once logged in, from the [Your Account](https://www.moo.com/account/) section you will find the option to edit your password along with other details including the account name or email address.

You will need to verify your existing password in order to change to a new one.

If you require assistance, please contact our [Customer Support Team.](https://support.moo.com/hc/en-us/articles/204288414-How-to-contact-our-Customer-Service-team)
