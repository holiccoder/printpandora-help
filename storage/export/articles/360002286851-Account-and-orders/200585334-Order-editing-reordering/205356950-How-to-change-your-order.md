**Editing an order**

We allow a *3-hour edit window* for design changes before we send your order to be printed. If you need to change the designs go to your [Order History](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/account/order-history.php) and click on the order you want to change.

**Please note that the editing window is not available on next day or 2-day delivery options.**

If the order is marked "Printing" or "Dispatched", it's usually too late to make changes. Finishing touches such as paper, quantity, and corner options cannot be altered once the order has been submitted.

**Adding to an order**

Once an order has been placed and confirmed, it is not possible to add additional items or adjust quantities. However, if you've already placed your order and would like additional items, please get in touch with our [Customer Service](http://{{CONTACT_URL}}) team and we'll see what we can do.
