### If you don't need to make any changes and just need more cards, then here's what you do.

1\. Visit the "[Reorder](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/account/reorder.php)" section and locate the pack you want to reorder. You will need to sign in if you aren't already.

2\. Next, find the item you want to reorder and choose the quantity in the dropdown.

3\. Click '**Reorder**' to add it to your cart.

![2017-04-17_1409.png]({{IMAGE_PENDING:29834768521876}})

### If you do need to make changes to your design or text, follow the steps below:

1\. Visit the "[Reorder](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/account/reorder.php)" section and locate the pack you want to reorder and change.

2\. Clicking "**Edit &amp; Reorder**" will bring you to the design workflow where you can edit directly on {{BRAND_NAME}} or upload new files accordingly.

3\. You can also use this option to select a different paper/finish, corner options, and quantity.
