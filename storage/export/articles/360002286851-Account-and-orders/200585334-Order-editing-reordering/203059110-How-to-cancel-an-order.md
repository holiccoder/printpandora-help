You can cancel an entire order from your [Order History](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/account/order-history.php) up to one hour after placing your order.

*\*(Please note, certain orders will not have this option).*

If you wish to cancel only certain items from your order or the option is not available from your account, please contact us and we'll do our best to help.

**Please note that this does not apply to Branded Merchandise (eg Custom Water Bottles, Custom Notebooks etc). For further information on Branded Merchandise policies, please refer** [**here**](https://{{HELP_DOMAIN}}/hc/en-us/sections/48009539966996)**.**
