You can change the delivery address of your order all the way up until it ships. To change it, you can go to [Y](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/account/)[our](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/account/)**[ Account](https://www.{{MAIN_DOMAIN}}/account/)**" and select **Order History**. Find the order you want to change and click **Edit shipping address**.

As long as the order hasn't been shipped already, it will go to the new address.
